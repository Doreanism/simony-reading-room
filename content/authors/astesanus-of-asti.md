Astesanus of Asti (died c. 1330) was a Franciscan friar from Asti in Piedmont and a canon lawyer. His major work, the *Summa de casibus conscientiae* (c. 1317), became one of the most widely circulated confessors' manuals of the later Middle Ages, surviving in numerous manuscripts and early printed editions. Drawing on both civil and canon law, it was valued as a practical reference for cases of conscience, with its sections *De significatione verborum* singled out for their treatment of legal terminology. He dedicated the work to Cardinal Giovanni Caetani Orsini.
