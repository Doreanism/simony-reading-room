An early thirteenth-century theological summa composed at Paris ca. 1213–1215 by Geoffrey of Poitiers (Gaufridus Pictaviensis), a master in the circle of Stephen Langton and Peter the Chanter. Following the order of Peter Lombard's *Sentences*, the work treats God, creation, the fall, the incarnation, the sacraments, and Christian ethics, drawing especially on Langton's lectures. BnF, MS Latin 3143 is a thirteenth-century manuscript copy in two-column gothic script with later modern foliation. The Summa has never been printed in full; only excerpts have appeared in print.
