The third volume of the Quaracchi critical edition of the *Summa Theologica* of Alexander of Hales (also known as the *Summa Halensis*), prepared by the Fathers of the Collegium S. Bonaventurae and published at Quaracchi (Florence) in 1930. This volume contains the second part of the second book (*Secunda Pars Secundi Libri*), comprising treatises on evil, sin, and the vices — including spoliatio gratuitorum, original sin, the capital vices, and the question of simony. A monumental thirteenth-century Franciscan synthesis assembled from the writings of Alexander and his school.
