William of Auxerre's *Summa aurea in quattuor libros Sententiarum*, an early scholastic systematic theology composed in the 1220s and arranged as a commentary on the four books of Peter Lombard's *Sentences*. Simony is treated in Book III in connection with the sacrament of orders. This *editio princeps* was printed at Paris by Philippe Pigouchet for the booksellers Nicolas Vaultier and Durand Gerlier on 3 April 1500, edited by the theologian Guillermus de Quercu, who also added the alphabetical index that follows the main text. This copy is from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Munich.
