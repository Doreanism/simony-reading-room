*The Obedience of a Christian Man* by William Tyndale, first published in 1528 and here in the corrected edition of 1535. The colophon bears the well-known false imprint — "At Marlborow in the lande of Hesse... by me Hans Luft," 29 October 1535 — under which Tyndale's books were actually printed in Antwerp. The work argues that secular rulers hold their authority directly from God and that subjects owe them obedience, while attacking the temporal power and "juggling" of the papacy and clergy. A foundational text of the English Reformation, it is reputed to have prompted Henry VIII to declare, "This book is for me, and all kings, to read."
