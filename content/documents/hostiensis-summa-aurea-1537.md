Henry of Susa's *Summa* (later known as *Summa aurea*), the great thirteenth-century commentary on the titles of the Decretals of Gregory IX. Drafted while Henry was archdeacon and bishop of Sisteron and revised in the 1250s, the work systematically treats each title of the *Liber extra* under the headings *quid sit, quare, qualiter, ubi, quando, per quem,* and *quid operetur*. It was the most consulted canonical *summa* of the later Middle Ages and earned its author the appellation *Hostiensis* from his cardinal-bishopric of Ostia. This Lyon 1537 edition was prepared with the summaries and annotations of Nicolaus Superantius and an alphabetical *repertorium*; printed by Theobald Paganus. Copy from the Biblioteca Universitaria di Bologna (Google Books).
