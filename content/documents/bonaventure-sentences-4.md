Volume IV of the Quaracchi critical edition of the *Opera Omnia* of St. Bonaventure (1889), containing his commentary on the fourth book of Peter Lombard's *Sentences*. Edited by the Friars Minor at the College of St. Bonaventure in Quaracchi (near Florence), this edition remains the standard scholarly text. Book IV treats the sacraments and the four last things.
