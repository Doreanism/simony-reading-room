Source collection of late-medieval Low German mystical and devotional texts edited by Rudolf Langenberg from manuscripts in Cologne, Münster, Hannover, and Wolfenbüttel. Its opening text is a Low German reconstruction of Geert Groote's lost treatise *De simonia ad beguttas* — a polemic against the buying and selling of admission to beguinages — followed by Low German hymns, the *Marienklage* of Dietrich Vrye, a fifteenth-century *Laienregel*, a treatise on spiritual unchastity (*De weltliche Minne*), and a Decalogue exposition from the Groote manuscript at Münster, together with Langenberg's notes and a concluding study on the relation of Low German mysticism to Meister Eckhart. Published at Bonn by P. Hanstein's Verlag.
