Angelus de Clavasio's *Summa angelica de casibus conscientiae*, an alphabetically arranged confessor's manual of cases of conscience first compiled in the 1480s. It treats moral and canonical questions — including a substantial entry on simony — in 659 articles ordered A–Z, drawing on the canon-law and theological tradition. This editio princeps was printed at Chivasso by Jacobino Suigo, 13 May 1486. This copy is from the Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale di Roma.
