The Decretum of Gratian (c. 1140) printed with the *glossa ordinaria*, edited under the authority of Pope Gregory XIII by the *correctores Romani* and issued at Venice in 1591. Each page presents Gratian's central canons and *dicta* in two columns surrounded by the *glossa ordinaria* — the standard medieval commentary compiled by Johannes Teutonicus around 1216 and revised by Bartholomaeus Brixiensis around 1245, as further corrected for the 1582 *editio Romana*. Each gloss is keyed to a lemma in the central text by a printed superscript letter. The volume contains the complete Decretum: the Distinctiones, the Causae, and *De Consecratione*. This copy is from the Bavarian State Library, digitized via Google Books.
