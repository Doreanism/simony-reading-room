Sylvester Prierias's *Summa summarum, quae Silvestrina nuncupatur*, an alphabetical Latin confessor's manual conceived as a "summa of summas." Compiled by the Dominican theologian Silvestro Mazzolini da Prierio (1456–1527), Master of the Sacred Palace under Leo X, it draws on the established confessor's-manual tradition (Pisanella, Astesana, Angelica) to treat moral and canonical cases of conscience in roughly 700 entries from *Abbas* to *Zelus*. First printed at Bologna in 1514–15, it went through more than forty editions before the close of the sixteenth century. This Lyon edition was printed by Théobald Payen (Theobaldus Paganus) in 1539; like the original, it is divided in two parts (*prima* and *secunda pars*), each followed by its own alphabetical *repertorium*.
