*The Floure of the Commaundementes of God* is an English translation of the French *La fleur des commandements de Dieu*, an anonymous late-medieval compilation of moral and devotional teaching organized around the Ten Commandments and illustrated with edifying exempla. The English version (traditionally ascribed to the translator Andrew Chertsey) was printed by Wynkyn de Worde; this is the 1521 edition.
