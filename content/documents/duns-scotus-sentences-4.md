Volume XIX of the Vivès edition of Duns Scotus's *Opera Omnia*, published in Paris in 1894 — a reprint, "accurately revised by the Franciscan Friars of the Observance," of Luke Wadding's 1639 Lyon edition. This volume contains the second half of Scotus's commentary on the fourth book of Peter Lombard's *Sentences*, distinctions 23 through 42, with the commentary of Antonius Hiquaeus (Antoine Hickey, OFM). Simony is treated in distinction 25. The "Wadding edition" remains the standard reference for those portions of Scotus's work that the modern Vatican critical edition has not yet reached.
