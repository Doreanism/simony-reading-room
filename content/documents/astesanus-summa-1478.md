Astesanus of Asti's *Summa de casibus conscientiae* (the "Summa Astesana"), a comprehensive confessor's manual completed c. 1317 and dedicated to Cardinal Giovanni Caetani Orsini. It became one of the most widely circulated penitential handbooks of the later Middle Ages. The work is divided into eight books: the divine precepts (the Decalogue), the virtues and vices, contracts and last wills, the sacraments of baptism, confirmation, and the Eucharist, penance and extreme unction, the sacrament of orders and ecclesiastical persons and things, ecclesiastical censures, and matrimony. Simony is treated at the end of Book VI, in the titulus *De simonia*. This *editio princeps* was printed at Venice by Johannes de Colonia and Johannes Manthen on 18 March 1478, edited by Bartolomeo Bellati and Gometius of Lisbon. Each book has its own folio numbering that restarts at I. This copy is from the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Munich.
