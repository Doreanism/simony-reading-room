scke/arayse ye the deed/hele ye the lepres chase awaye the deuylles/ye haue taken it for nothynge gyue you it for nothyng. Also it is wryten. Administre ye takynge nothynge for it the Eucharistie that is the sacrament of the aulter unto the people/confesse ye takynge nothynge/baptyse ye takynge nothynge/gyue you unto all folke for nothynge after the appostle. Unde augustinus dicit. Gratis eucharistiam plebi ministrate:gratis confitemini: gratis baptisate secundum apostolum:cunctis gratis date.

Whan ony preest selleth or marchaundeth the masses or that he synge masse for moneye and that the cause fynall of the dyslectacyon be to thende that he haue the sayd moneye/it is symonye/and synneth mortally. Unde augustinus. Qui precise unam missas vel plures per pecunijs celebrat damnationem sibi eternam preparat. He that syngethe a masse/or many for moneye he prepareth unto hymself eternall dampnacyon. And it is here to be noted that the thynges temporelles ne shold neuer be the ende of thynges spyrytuelles/but there be causes well mouynge. Unde hec est falsa. The preest is holden to gyue the thynges spyrytuelles to thende that he haue the thynges temporelles. Sed hec est vera. The laye man is holden to gyue the thynges temporelles for the thynges spyrytuelles/not byenge them / for they sholde not be solde as it is sayd in lyke wyse as a man selleth the flesshe upon the stalle. The grace of god the whiche is in the sacrament is in apprecyable and it falleth not under ony pryce/for man hathe not thynge whereof he it may bye/but for asmoche as the seruauntes of god ne may laboure in thynges spyrytuelles whout hauynge their lyuynge et sustentacyon / god wyll that a man unto them gyue some thynge for to lyue whan
