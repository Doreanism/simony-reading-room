We rede math. r. How our lorde sayd. Gratias date/the whiche is expounded in two maners. Or whan a man there putteth no cause of sale/or whan a man hath no regarde in gyuynge but alonly god/or only grace. And therfore he the whiche gyueth vnto his kinnesman ne gyueth in suche wyse/albeit that god hathe sayd/gratis date. Also who so wyl gyue vnto his kynnesman withoute symonye/it behoueth that the persone be worthy it/And that the gyfte be made for the loue of god purely/ as it is sayd. Ut intret per ostium: id est christum in ouile ouium. Our lorde Jesu cryste gaue the dygnyte and gouernement of the chirche vnto saynt peter and not vnto saynt Johan nor vnto his other parentes/in gyuynge vs example that in lyke wyse shold we do. Also whan the appostles chose Mathyas and Joseph the iuste/ to thende that one of them two were apostle in the place of iudas. Sors cecidit super mathia. For Joseph was of the parente of Jesus. Legitur in secundo libro regum. Quia infidelis est episcopus qui nepotulo suo cofert prelationem cum sciat enim inabilem et sciat totum pondus prelii uersum super prelatum. That is to say. The bisshoppe is vnfaythfull the whiche bryngeth vnto prelacyon/or dygnyte his lytel neue we whan it is so that he knoweth that he is feble to fyghte and that he knoweth that all the countre and the ruyne of the batayll tourneth on the prelates.
