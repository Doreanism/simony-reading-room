But in fygure of all those that selleth or byeth thynges spyrytualles he et his moneye was cursed by the mouth of saynt peter/et bycause that he was the fyrst in the newe testament that wolde by thynge spyrytuell this espyce or kynde of theft is named symonye in all holy chyrche bycause of the cursed symon fygured of all the dampned symonyacques. For to sel or to bye a thynge spyrytuall a man sholde vnderstande euery paccyon or contracte that which is not made symply et purely for the loue of god/but is made pryncypally by gyftes prayers/seruyces/et for ony other thynge temporell. Also for to sell or to bye thynges spyrytuel it is here to vnderstade the grace of the holy ghost/et the vertues that ben in the soule the spyryte of prophecye/offyce of predycacyon/or to do the offyce dyuyne in the chyrche et to adminystre the holy sacramentes. By thynges annexed vnto the spyrytualyte is to be vnderstade sepultures/dysmes/chalyces/corporaces/towelles aubes/et other besselles/or vestymentes halowed that which may be solde or chauged vnto other chirches not the more dere for as moche as it is halowed other wyse it sholde be symonye. Also cures/prebedes/chanonryes/chapelles/ryght of patronage in it selfe symply consydered tyghte to receyue dysmes/oblacyons bycause of benefyce of the chirche/auctoryte or offyce to serue a cure or other prebede of the chirche/al these thynges haue annexacyon vnto holy ordres et they ne may be solde after the forme before sayd but that there be symonye which is a cryme capytal after the droitz et compared vnto heresye. vt habet.i.ca.q.i.
