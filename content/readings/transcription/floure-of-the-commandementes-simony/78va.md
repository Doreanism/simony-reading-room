# Of symony that god defendeth

After the maysters symonye is in suche wyse dyffinyed. Simonia est studiosa voluntas emendi vel vendendi spirituale vel annexum spirituali. A man commytteth symonye for to sell/or to bye a spyrytual thynge or a thynge that is annexed vnto the spyrytuallyte by paccyon of gyfte/or promesse/seruyce/prayers/supplycacyons vndue/or by ony other thynge temporell. God et nature gaynsayth that a thynge spyrytuell may be compared/or bought by thynge corporell et temporell of what pryce et dygnyte that it be/as it shal be declared here after. This synne is named symonye/for as moche as in the tyme of the appostles the fals et dysloyall symon magus offred a grete some of moneye vnto the appostles wenynge by that too gete the grace
