For in the courtes of prelates a man sholde proue the clerkes. Also to ordeyne charyte a man sholde gyue fyrst vnto his famylyers as vnto straugers yf they ben egally good.

The people saye the whiche gyueth or prompteth or maketh praiers et supplicacyons indue for to benefyce theyr kynnesmen/seruauntes or other theyr frendes/ben symonyakes and sholde be accursed/after that that saynt Thomas sayeth and symode. Yf ony hath take dygnyte epyscopale or sacerdotale led by auaryce/by loue/seruyce or prayers/and he ne hathe left it in his lyfe/and at the dethe ne hathe founde it in sharpe penaunce he shal pay in pardurabylyte. In gyuyg a benefyce vnto one the whiche is of his blode/albeit there be no symonye for that that there is no sale/yet is there synne the whiche is compt in thre maners/or for asmoche that he loueth tenderly his flesshe/or for that that he coueyteth to gyue a thynge indue vnto his kynnesman/or for asmoche as he bylependeth the thynge spyriten in gyuynge it vnto one indigne. And it semeth that he sholde haue therin symony pfye hadde there garde how his cosyn hathe the profyte temporall for the spyrytuall in as moche as cosyn for he sholde rescepur a thynge the whiche ne sholde be due vnto hym.
