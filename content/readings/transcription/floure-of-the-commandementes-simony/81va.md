and the scyence and the doctryne in the eloquence & worde of wysdome. An aduocate ought to be verytable & to susteyne veryte. Also he ought to kepe & defende the iust cause that it peryllshe not. And afterwarde he may take gyft for the traueyll of his body as it is sayd without to excede the boundes of reason as many done the whiche demaunde and take salayres and gyftes outrageously / more than they ne sholde / & that they ne haue deserued too haue. And therfore they sholde drede dampnacyon / for they ne ben iuste & accomplyssshe not iustyce in theselfe to be in wyl to haue the thynges that they haue not wonne & the whiche ne apperteyneth vnto them.

Also they sholde drede yf they haue hated & broken Iustyce / iugement / the good cause & the good ryght to receyue yeftes. For they synne mortally & renne in maladyccyon. Unde / Isaie.v. Ve qui iustificates impium pro muneribus: et iustitiam iusti aufertis ab eo. Also they sholde drede yf they haue no wyl to lede / to consayl to kepe the causes of the poores / as they done those of the ryche / for that the they haue no goodes to gyue vnto theym. It is wryten eccle. In iudicado esto pupillis misericors vt pater et eris tu velut altissimi filius obediens et miserebit tibi. In iugement be thou mercyfull vnto the orphelyns in lykewyse as a fader and thou shalt be obedyent. And also as the sone of the ryght hye god / & he shall haue mercy on the. Also yf these aduocates haue made the ylle causes good by they cautelles / malyces & subtyltees / and maketh to lese the good ryght they synne and ben vniuste and corrupters of iustice.
