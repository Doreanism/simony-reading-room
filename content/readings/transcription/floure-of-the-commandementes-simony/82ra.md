et augu. dicit. Non debimus euangelizare vt viuam: sed viuere vt euangelizem. we sholde not preche the gospel to the ende that we lyuen/ but to lyue to the ende that we preche. Also we sholde preche to the ende that the rewarde of predicacyon be not the mete the whiche is appetyted a desyred/ but the thynge necessarye the whiche folowith/ that is sustentacion vnto the temporalites the whiche is requysyte vnto a precher. Item augu. Euangelizare debemus vt non sit merces predicationis cibus qui appetit: sed necessariu quod sequitur id est substantatio in temporalibus que necessaria est predicatori. Also those clerkes that letteth to preche/ to shewe/ to reprehende/ to correcte the vyces for ony fauours/ or for false loue/ or for drede/ or for any other thynge inutyle and also holde they peas to se and to here that pl. The prophete ysaye calleth them dogges not wyllynge to barke/ that is to preche. An ysaye. ibi. Canes muti no valentes letrare. Sequitur. Oes in via sua declinauerit vnusquisqs ad auaritiam sua a sumo vsqs ad nouissimu et. A dogge that which bayeth not for to sustepne his mayster is not good vnto hym/ no more is a precher vnto god the whiche is dombe to systeyne verite. And therfore he is called dogge. Et legit hiere. iiij. a sapientes sunt vt faciant mala/ bene autem facere nescierit. They ben wyse to do yll but not to do good. Example in the gospel of hym the whiche hydde the talent he was called an yll seruaunte and a slouthfull/ et was comaunded that men sholde take away frome hym the talente. Vnde math. trb. Auferte ab ille talentu. And for als moche as the habondaunce of the derkenes of synne hath ouerclouded the veryte of the comaundementes of god in the more parte of the crysten people lyuynge at this daye.
