G. Also men rede in secudo machabeorum. iiij. how that kynge Anthiochus solde vnto Iason the dygnyte to be souerayne preest (that is bysshop) et yll came vnto hym. The prophete ysaye sayth in his .v. chapptre. Ue qui iustificatis impium, pro muneribus et iustitiam iusti aufertis ab eo. Cursed be you the whiche iustyfye the yll by gyftes/and take away the iustyce of the iuste. Those the whiche entreth in to benefyces vnduely by the force and puyssaunce of prynces or greate lordes/or by menaces/murmures/or by theyr prayers and supplycacyon/et not by the dore that whiche is Ihesu cryste ben theues. for the drede puyssauce or fauoure of sayd lordes that good ryght is corrupte/the good ben expelled/and the vnworthly beneficed et yll cometh vnto them. We haue an example in primo machabeorum. vij. how cursed Alchymus was costytute souerayne bysshop of the kynge demetrius. The whiche dyd many of ylles. Also the sayd Alchimus deyed cursedly/as it is wryten. i. machabeorum. ix. ca. This exaple is wryten after. Que. lxxxv. h.

C. Also some entreth into benefyce vnduely by flateryes glosynges et decepcyons. And for as moche as they entre not by the dore that is by Ihesu cryst they ben theuys.
