whan the wyfe of zebede aunte of Jesus hym prayed that her two chyldren/saynt James et saynt Johan cosyns of our lorde that he sholde make them sytte the one on his ryght hande and the other on his lefte hande. The answer was. Nescitis quid petatis. They were unworthy to be set nere by hym. And therfore god sayd unto her ye wote not what ye demaunde. Marye ye may wellendure passyon as J. But to sette you on my ryght hande it is not unto me to gyue it you:sed quibus paratum est a patre meo. That is unto them the whiche ben dygne.

Fourthly those the whiche make paccion to gyue certayne somme for to be receued in a monystery suche wyse rented that it is suffycyent to susteyne et to puruaye as well those that there ben nowe as those that they shall recepue they ben symonyakes And in lyke wyse those the whiche maketh the sayd recepcyon/or therto gyue counsayl or consentement/et there nys custome unto the contrary the whiche maye excuse the synne.

Fyfthly the people of the chirche sholde admynystre the sacramentes/and the sepultures without makynge paccyon ne exaccyon of money/otherwyse there is symonye. But after that they haue done their deuoyre the laye people them sholde content after the customes auncyentes or otherwyse a man may make them to come before the bysshop after the droitz. Extra De symonia ad apostolicam. The sacramentes of the chirche ne sholde be solde/for that sholde be symonye. Oure lorde it shewed clerely unto his apostles whan he sayd unto them Jund math.x.
