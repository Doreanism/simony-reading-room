in partyculer the cautelous decepcion of this comdampned synne of symonye. But these thynges spoken here shortly suffiseth vnto people of good conscyence to make them enquyre more playnly after the occasyons et dyuers maners the whiche vnto them maye in this thynge come vpon or happen. et. More ouer people symonyaces sholde drede maledyccyon and excommunycacyon. Also they sholde drede irregularyte the whiche shall not be declared by cause of shortnes. Also they sholde drede to be founde in the iugement before god. Also they sholde drede to lese the ioyes of paradyse. Also they sholde drede punycyon and dampnacyon. Example of a man dampned that whiche had many benefyces et. Quere. lxxxv. e. Another exaple how fewe of curatis ben saued. Quere. lvi. g. Also they sholde drede to be sente for to abyde in the fyre of hell perpetually. Those the whiche hath offended by symonye/ tenne they vnto penaunce they shall haue grace and mercye. et. Also doo they of almesdedes for that is a good remedye.
