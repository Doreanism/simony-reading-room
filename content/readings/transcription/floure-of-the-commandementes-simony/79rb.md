Secondely a clerke that maketh paccyon with a man of the chyrche or other for to haue his tytle in promysynge that he shall neuer demaunde nothynge for asmoche as vnto hym suffyseth that it may passe/he is pryued from the orders that he receyueth in suche maner that none there may dyspence hym but the [pope] after the droite. Extra de simonia per tuas.

Thyrdly two persones benefyced the whiche couenanteth togyder vnder suche forme(permute we benefyces)but for asmoche as your benefice is better than myne I shal gyue you suche a somme of mony This is here symonye. How be it that the one may make pencion vnto the other wout symonye. And yf they make the sayd permutacyon symply wout ony thynge demaundynge the one of the other/but they make gyftes or promesses vnto the patron for to graunte his good wyll therunto it is also in this wyse for asmoche as we may not permytte togyder wout the consent of the byshop labour we bothe to gete his consente Suche paccyon ne sholde be symonyacall yf it were made symply wout gyfte/and the sayd consentement were not goten by maner of symonye. ¶ Also to renounce vnto ony benefyce by suche condycyon/et paccyon made that it sholde be gyuen vnto suche a persone it is symonye/whan the renouncyacyon is not made vnto a persone that is worthy to haue it/et symply and purely for the loue of god. Legitur ioh.x. Ego sum ostium ouitium per me si quis introierit saluabitur et pascua inuenit. Our lorde Jhesus sayth. I am the dore of the shepecote yf ony there entre by me he shall be saued et he shall fynde pasture there within. Symonye is commytted and done somtyme as well of the persone
