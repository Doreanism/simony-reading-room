so doynge the aduocate yeldeth hymselfe culpable / & bryngeth hymselfe in with the malefactoure. By the whiche he is dygne of punycyon & synneth mortally. Unde paulus ad roma.i. Non soluz qui faciut peccata: sed qui consentiut facientibus digni sunt morte. Not alonely those the whiche do the synnes / but those the whiche also consenteth vnto the doers / ben worthy of dethe. Those synneth mortally / how be it the a manne sholde vnderstande that the sayd aduocates comytteth symony. &c.

Example how the deuyll bare away in body and in soule a cursed coueytous thefe aduocate. Querr.lxxxi.c. Another crample of an aduocate insacyable the whiche ledde a cause vnto a man the whiche ne dyde brynge vnto hym money vnto his appetyte and he wronged hym and toke the proces and brake of the lettres / and trode vpon it by dispyte in saynge. Vyllayn there is thy procesle the whiche ne broughtest me but this. The good man in weppynge excused hym by pouerte. But to be short anone after the aduocate deyed myscheuously. Also the mayster of a scole ne sholde sell the scyence but the labour the he taketh of his body. And i lykewyse of other seblables. Quia omnis operarius dignus est mercede sua: vt dictum est. Also the prechers ne sholde sell the scyence that they speke in prechynge for the cause fynall to haue the gayne temporall / or in lykewyse for to haue fauour humayne or for vayn glorye / and not for charyte and reconsylyacyon of the poore synners / commyttin symony. But for as moche as they haue had payne corporall for to studye / to preche & to walke they may take that / that men and women vnto them gyue for the susteytacyon of they luynge. Quia deus ordinauit his qui euangelium annuciant de euangelio viuere, vt dicitur. corinth.
