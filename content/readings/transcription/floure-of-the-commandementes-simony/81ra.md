They the whiche on the alter dysseruen shall be parteyners of the aulter/ in suche wyse that god hath ordeyned vnto theym the whiche shewe the gospell/ to lyue of the gospell/ not that they shal selle it/ for that sholde be symonye as it is sayd.

Syxtely marke well that no man laye ne sholde presume to do the offyce of preestes & which ben in holy ordres/ where yt they do yll shall come vnto theym as dyde vnto the kynge Ozyas. For after that he had coueyted and presumed to do the offyce and that he had taken the enseter & encensed and menaced the preestes for that they gaynsayd hym leperye hym to be in the vylage within the house of god/ &c.as it is wryte.ij.paralipo.xxvi. Quere.lxxxv.g.

Seuenthly a manne sholde selle the yeftes of grace/ ne the vertues the whiche ben in soule ne sepence/ where that sholde be symonye. Augustin dicit. Spectat ad officium vestre dignitatis gratis pete tib dona dare gratis:sed si vni fidei munera vedatis in cursores giezi lepra vos sciatis. It belongeth vnto offyce of your dygnyte to gyue gyftes for nothynge vnto the demaundauntes of grace/ but yf ye selle in ony wyse the gyftes of fayth knowlege you than vnto the lepre of Gyezy. In lyke wyse as sayth saynt austen vnto the mynystres of the chyrche. Gyezye was the dyscyple of Helyseus/ the whiche toke a grete gyfte of the ryche man Naaman for that his mayster had heled hym of his lepye. And therfore the sayd prophete Helyseus sayd vnto gyezy.
