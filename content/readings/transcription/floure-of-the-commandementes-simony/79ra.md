## Of the maners of symonye that god defendeth

It is wryten actuum.viij.ca. Pecunia tua tecum sit in perditionem Saynt peter sayd vnto symon magus. Thy moneye be with the in perdycyon. These wordes here maye be taken and also vnderstande vpon all symonyacques as it is before sayd/but a man sholde vnderstande that the synne of symonye is chaunged in to many maners after the dyuersyte of the people the whiche entermell them vnduely in thynges in spyrytuell thynges/as hereafter some in partyculer shall be declared.

Fyrst a man the whiche receyueth holy ordres or the whiche entreth in ony benefyce of the chyrche in gyuynge or in promettynge by hymselfe or by other/golde or syluer or ony other thynge is suspende by right wryten Extra de symonia.tanta t.i.ca.i.q. Reperiuntur. And yf in suche estate he misbehaue than vnto the mysteres dyuynes he is irreguliere et may not be assoylled yf he ne resyne the benefyce/or yf he haue not dyspensacyon of [the pope].
