A question to vnderstode yf an aduocate may sell the sceyence of the iust cause that he demeneth. The answere. He ne sholde selle the sceyence/ but he may selle the labour and trauayll of his body he taketh to studye to plede and to defende the iust cause. for god sayeth in the gospell that the workman is worthy of his mete and of his hire. Mathei.x. Dignus est enim operarius cibo suo. Et luce.x. Dignus est enim operatius mercede sua. But yf the aduocate solde the sceyence by maner of sale/ or that he had in his thought the entencyon to selle it he sholde be symonyacke before god. It is wryten prouerbiorum.xxiij. Veritatem eme et nole vendere sapientiam et doctrinam et intelligentiam.&c. Beye true and sell not wysdom doctryne and intellygece.

Also the scyence solde noyeth as it is sayd. and yf it be gyuen it groweth and also multeplyeth/ And yf it be hydde it serueth of nothynge/ Unde scientia que data est crescit\:crescere nescit si taceatur. Et legitur ecclesi.xx. Sapientia absconsa est et thesaurus invisus: que vtilitas in vtrisqp. Sequitur Melior est qui celat insipientiam suam homo qui abscondit sapientiam suam. Sapyence hydde is a treasoure where a man seeth not what profyte is in the one & in the other. Better is he the whiche hydeth his vnwysdome than the man the whiche hydeth his sapience. Et legitur eccle.iij. Non abscondas sapientiam tuam in decore eius in lingua enim agnoscitur sapientia:& sensus et scientia et doctrina in verbo sensati.
