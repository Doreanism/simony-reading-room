we will make an ensample of you. Oh how terreble are they. Come and swere (saye they) that ye wilbe obediente vnto oure indictions. And bi that craft wringe thei their purses and make them droppe as longe as there is a peny in them. In thre or foure yeres shall they in those offices gete ynough to paye for a bisshopes bulles. What other thinge are these in a realme saue horslieches and euen very magottes cancres and caterpillers/which deuoure no moare but all that is greane and those wolues whom Paul prophesied shuld come and shuld not spare the flocke Actes.xx.chap. And which Christ said shuld come in lambes skynnes and bade vs beware of them and iudge them bi their workes.
