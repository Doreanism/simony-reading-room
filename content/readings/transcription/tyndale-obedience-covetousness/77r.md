when corne is ripe. They will forgette nothinge. No man shall dye in their dett/or if any man doo/he shall paye it when he is deed. They will loose nothinge. Why? It is Gods/it is not thers. It is sent Luders rentes/sent Albans londes/sent Edmonds ryght/sent Peters patrimonye saye they/and none of oures. Item if a man dye in a nother mans paresh/bysides that he must paye at home a mortuary for forgoten tythes he must there paye also the best that he there hath. It is moch veryly for so litle payne takynge in confession and in ministringe the sacramentes. The beedrolles.
