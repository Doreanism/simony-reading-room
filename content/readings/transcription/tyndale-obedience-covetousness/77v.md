Offeringes at prestes fyrst masses. Item man is professed/of what so euer religion it be/but he must bringe some what. The halowinge or rather coniuringe of churches chapels/altares/super altares/chalice vestimentes and belles. Then boke/bell/candelsticke/organes/chalice/vestimentes copes/altare clothes/syrpleses: towels basens/ewars/shippe/senser and all maner ornamentes must be founde them frely/they will not geue a myte there vnto. Last of all what swarmes of begginge freres are there. The person shereth the vicare shaueth/the parish prest polleth/the frere scrapeth and the pardoner pareth we lacke but a bocher to pole of the skynne.

What gete they in their spirituall law (as they call it) in a yere/at the arches and in euery dioses/what gete the Comissaries and officials with their somners and apparitars by bawdery in a yere? Shall ye not fynde curates ynowe which to flatter the comessaries and officials with all that they may/ye goo quite them selues shall ope vnto them the cofessios of the richest of their pareshes Whom they cite priuely and laye to their charges secretly. Yf they desyre to knowe their accusers/naye saye they/the mater is knowe well ynough and to moare then ye are ware of. Come laye youre hande on the boke/yf ye forswere youre selfe/we shall bringe proues/we will handle you/
