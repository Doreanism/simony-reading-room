suppose we in a litle or all to gether the third fote of all the temporall londes. Marke well how many personages or vicariages ar ther in the realme that at the lest haue a plowe londe a pece. Then note the londes of Bisshopes/Abbotes/Pryors/Nunnes/knyghtes of saynt Johns/Cathedrall chyrches/Coleges/Chauntries/Frechapels. For though the house fall in decaye and the ordinaunce of the founder be lost/yet will not they loose the londes. What cometh ones in/maye neuer more out. They make a frechapell of it/so that he weneth it shall to nought therfore. Besides all this/how many chaplaines doo gentlemen fynde at their awne cost in their houses. How mani singe for soules by testamentes. Then the prouinge of testamentes and presinge of goodes the Bisshope of Caunterburyes prerogatyue. Ys that not moch thorow the Realme in a yere? Foure offeringe dayes and preuey tythes. There is no seruante/but that he shall paye some what of his wages. None shall receaue the bodi of Christ at Ester/be he neuer so pore a begger or neuer so yonge a lad or mayd: but they must paie some what for it. Then mortuaries for forgote tythes (as they saye) And yet what parsone or vicare is there that wil forgete to haue a pygyn howse to pecke vp some what both at sowinge tyme and at heruest
