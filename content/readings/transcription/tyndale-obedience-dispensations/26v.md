Moreouer when she is geue by the iudge to the one partie and also maried euen then afterwardes shall the contrarie parte sue before an hyer iudge or an other that succeadeth the same and for money deuorce her againe. So shamfully doeth the couetousnes and ambicion of our prelates mocke with the lawes of god.
