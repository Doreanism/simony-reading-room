arate vnseparable matrimonie. For after thou art lawfully maryed at the commaundemente of father & mother & with the consent of all thy frendes: yet if thou wilt be desgysed like vnto one of them & swere obedience to their tradicions/ thou mayst disobeye father & mother / breake the othe which thou hast sworne to God before his holy congregacion & withdraw loue & charite the hiest of gods commaundementes and that dutye and seruice which thou owest to thy wyfe, wherof Christ can not dispence with the. For Christ is not against god/ but with God & came not to breake gods ordinaunces/ but to fulfill them. That is he came to overcome the with kidnes & to make the to do of very loue the thinge which the law compelleth the to do. For loue only and to do seruice to thy neyghbour is the fulfillinge of the law in the sight of God. To be a monke or a frere/ thou mayst thus forsake thy wife before thou hast lyne with her/ but not to be a seculer prest. And yet after thou art professed/ the Pope for money will dispence with the/ both for thy cote & all thi obedience/ & make a seculer prest of the: likewise as it is simony to sell a benefice (as thei call it) but to resigne apon a pencion & then to redeme the same/ is no simony at all. Oh craftie iugglers & mockers with the worde of god.
