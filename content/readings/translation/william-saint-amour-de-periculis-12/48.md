Likewise 1 Thessalonians 2, *Nor have we ever been on any occasion of avarice, though we might have been burdensome unto you, as the Apostles of Christ*; the *Glossa*: He calls the due owed to apostolic power a BURDEN on account of the Pseudo-Apostles, who, usurping it unduly, were exacting it importunately from the populace. Augustinus also says this expressly in his book *De opere Monachorum*, speaking of certain regulars who wished to live by the Gospel, in these words: But these our brethren rashly arrogate to themselves (so I judge) that they have this kind of power — namely, of living by the Gospel; for if they are evangelists, if they are ministers of the altar, if they are dispensers of the sacraments, then, I grant, they have it. As though he were saying: if they do not hold these offices, neither do they have the power of living by the Gospel. But it is established that none hold these offices except those who have a populace subject to them, as has been shown above. Likewise 1 Thessalonians 5, *We beseech you, brethren, to know* — that is, to show that you know — *THEM WHO LABOR AMONG YOU* — that is, in your governance and in teaching — *AND ARE OVER YOU* in dignity; *and this in the Lord* — that is, by the Lord’s ordinance. To these alone, therefore, are stipends owed: those who preside in the rule of souls and in the dignity of office.

That such persons are not permitted to live by curiosity — that is, by attending to the affairs of others — is plain from the *Apostolus*, who says in 2 Thessalonians 3, *For we were not disorderly among you*; the *Glossa*: like those who attend to other people’s business, wandering about hither and thither. And further on, *For we have heard that some among you walk disorderly, working not at all, but busying themselves with curiosity* — the *Glossa*: with the affairs of others; and in this manner they earn their keep. Such conduct is abhorrent to the Lord’s discipline; for their God is their belly, who provide for themselves what they need by a base meddling. Whence such persons can truly be said to involve themselves in secular business, since from such meddling they often gather large sums of money, against the *Apostolus*, who says in 2 Timothy 2, *No man, being a soldier to God, entangleth himself with secular businesses*; the *Glossa*: That business is secular when the mind is occupied with the care of gathering money without bodily labor; for it is care, not the hands, that does the work.
