We reply: No; since they act against the *Apostolus* and against other Scriptures; and length of time does not diminish sins, but increases them, *Extra De Simonia, cap. Non satis*. Wherefore, even if it had been confirmed by the Church through error, nonetheless, once the truth has been discovered, it ought to be revoked. For we do not deny that a sentence of the Roman See can be changed for the better, *35. q. 9. cap. Sententiam*. And just as truth, the more it is shaken about, the more it shines forth into the light; so too a pernicious thing, when called back into judgment, is condemned more gravely and without repentance — that is, with greater severity, such that there is no need to repent thereafter; for it is a divine fruit that justice be more often re-examined; thus is it read at *35. q. 9. cap. Graue*. And as to what some say, that it is of greater perfection to have nothing, neither in private nor in common, than to have nothing of one’s own but to have things in common, as the Monks do — this is contrary to the truth, since Prosper says at *12. q. 1. cap. 13*, *It is expedient that the resources of the Church be possessed*; and below, *By which it is sufficiently shown both that private goods ought to be despised for the sake of perfection, and that without any impediment to perfection the Church can possess resources, which are indeed common*; therefore for the Regular Church to have common possessions does not impede, nor diminish, its perfection. Thus, then, it manifestly appears from what has been said that it is permitted neither to a Preacher nor to any other Christian to beg perpetually, if he is able to obtain the necessities of life from elsewhere without sin. It is also clear that by the removal of begging, or of seeking the necessities of life, the usurpation of preaching is taken away from the Pseudo-preachers. And thus it is plain in what way the dangers which threaten the Church through those who creep into houses, and through other Pseudo \[-preachers\], and through the Idle, the Curious, and the Wandering, can be repelled from the Church.
