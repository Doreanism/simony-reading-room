In three or four years they will get enough in those offices to pay for a bishop’s bulls. What else are these in a realm but horse-leeches, and indeed the very maggots, cankers, and caterpillars that devour everything green and nothing else—and those wolves whom Paul prophesied would come and would not spare the flock (Acts 20), and whom Christ said would come in lambs’ skins, bidding us beware of them and judge them by their works.
