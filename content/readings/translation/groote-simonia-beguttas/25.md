And the higher and more inward and divine a virtue is — the more spiritual it is — as faith, hope, and love, which are the most spiritual of all, since they join us and bind us to God without any intermediary. Likewise, virtues stand above virtues, the one above the other, according to their degree and their offices; and they all exist for the sake of love, and in love they unite us with God and with the Holy Spirit. Thus Gregory says in the homily on the words *hoc est praeceptum meum*: just as many branches come forth from one root, so many virtues are born of one love; the branch of good work has nothing green about it if it does not abide in the root of love; and into this all virtues press, so as to be united with God and to cleave to God and to become one spirit with him; and this is the nearest and greatest spirituality that a person can have here. Every virtue is a gift and work of the Holy Spirit and makes a person spiritual, and with every virtue the Holy Spirit dwells in the spiritual person. Thereby it is proved that all the works and exercises of the virtues are spiritual works and exercises; for what is born of the Spirit is spirit, and what is born of the flesh is flesh, as Christ says. Indeed, the works and exercises of the virtues are, to my thinking, more spiritual than the works of miracles, when they come from a greater and more spiritual heart and from a more spiritual ground and carry one nearer to the Spirit. Nevertheless, to sell the works of miracles is simony, and from this simony first took its name and was first described in Scripture, as Gehazi did, who took garments from Naaman — who was leprous — because he had been made well (17b) by his master Elisha, as stands in the Book of Kings; and likewise Simon Magus. From him simony has its name. In him there was a twofold wickedness: to desire the work and power of miracles, as the teachers say, and to wish to have, for money, the power to give the Spirit to another in visible signs, so that he might then sell the miracles and from them get money.
