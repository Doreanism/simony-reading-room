These are the words of the masters, written out word for word, with which other teachers agree. Now mark this. If it is simony in such a lay life and worldly state, whenever some spiritual thing is attached to it, what then must it be in the case of that life which is wholly ordered to God and to a spiritual appearance and \[set apart\] from the world? Further, I said at the outset that, if to your convent or to the place within the convents any other spiritual things are bound up, or follow upon it, then the simony is greater or lesser. (6b) In the first place: if the third order is attached to it, so that everyone who enters there must become a member of the third order, then it is open simony to give or to promise a sum of money, or to bring in as a contribution the very property that one possesses. For although the third order is not a complete religious order, it is nevertheless a manner of life that has something in common with religion. And holy Church receives it and approves it. So says Iohannes Andree in *clementinis*, and Saint Thomas in the *secunda secundae*, and other teachers. And if this third order were attached \[to a convent\] in such a way that a person entered some convent and committed such simony, then it seems to me that the person who had committed such simony — whether such persons be many or few or all of them — ought to give up that place and take up penance in some stricter order or in some stricter manner of life. So speak the decretals concerning religious persons, *extra de simonia* in the chapter *Cum simoniacum*, and likewise Thomas and the common teachers. And these go on to speak of other matters with distinctions: what one ought to do if there were no stricter order, or if one could not do this without great anxiety of soul, or if the persons might become wayward — but there is no need to write of these things at this time, since you say that the third order is not attached to it. But let us leave the third order to your own conscience. Let us instead consider the state of the beguines in general.
