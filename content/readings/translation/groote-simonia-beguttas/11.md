Likewise, what is this — that on such grounds a person is excluded because he has followed Christ’s counsel and sold all his goods and given them to the poor? And alas! what is it that on such grounds one should be turned away from your community, when all religious orders hold as a third strand of perfection precisely this: poverty together with obedience and chastity? Likewise, dear people, if Saint Clare or Saint Elizabeth were to come in poverty, by your statutes they would be excluded. And alas, before God it is just as truly excluded when in our intent and according to our statutes we are willing to shut them out should they come — for that is all done in the heart and before God, just as Christ speaks concerning unchastity.

Now consider, then, how far all poor saints are from you — indeed, all rich saints in will. For had they nothing, you would take nothing from them; if you did take them in, you would not take them in for the holy name’s sake or because they were disciples of Christ or holy, but only because they could give goods. Truly, truly, the name shall answer for you in eternity, in the recompense by which God shall pay the greedy. And who upon earth can absolve you of this unless you turn entirely from these things? I take your own conscience as witness. Likewise, tell me, if some thoroughly good friend, who had given you all his honor and earthly and temporal goods, also placed in trust a piece of property, that you should preserve it for him and have it preserved by the one who would keep it for him most honorably and most fruitfully — how could you, with any good conscience, entrust the building or keeping of a house or estate to a person for the sake of his blood-kin or his goods, when, by your own estimation or by the teaching of the wise and by common custom, he is not accustomed to build it so well, nor to keep the house so well, nor so honorably, (10b) as another better person could? Could you regard, in such an entrustment, blood or wealth — which hindered the safekeeping more than it helped — or even if it did not hinder, still you ought not look to it except insofar as it brought benefit? Just so it stands with your house or prebend, which God has entrusted to you to give in the end to those who will possess it more honorably, more virtuously, and more fruitfully, in order to walk toward God, according to the estimation of your conscience and according to the estimation of the teaching of Christ.
