But hearts are all alike evil before God, and what is in truth before God matters far more than how things stand in the \[outward\] account. And however little spirituality clings to it, it is surely a great evil thus to receive people with \[prior\] agreements or with the intention of worldly gain. For Christ says, as is written in Luke: “He who is evil in a small matter, is also evil in a greater.” Furthermore, it must now be known that, out of a hundred thousand simonies which now occur in churches or in prebends, not one is judged. I have in my days never seen \[a case of\] simony judged externally, and this comes about because the Church, in her tribunals, seldom — and far too seldom — is willing to hear and to receive the articles brought against simoniacs, in order to avoid a greater evil, of which it would be too long to write here; 1) and for this reason every person remains in his simony, and simony becomes very widespread everywhere. And know that no custom or practice, even were the whole world to do it, can grant license to any simony or to any evil intention inwardly. Furthermore, even if there were no external law, let us look at the \[eternal\] law which is written in our heart by nature and by grace. The foundation of all foulness and of all simony in the heart is this: that a person chooses temporal and outward goods above spiritual good, or above eternal good, or above things which bring reward in eternity. Where is the person who is truly believing, and who stands in true hope, who would wish to be deprived of the eternal reward of one Our Father or of one good desire for the sake of any temporal good — yes, for a thousand thousand marks? All upright hearts must say that they would not do so inwardly, apart from any external teaching, for the eternal law of God shows this, and our heart bears witness to it.
