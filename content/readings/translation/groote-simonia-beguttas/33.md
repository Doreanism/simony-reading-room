But the *Book of Cologne* would divide the *religiones* and the things by their names; nevertheless they all come from the virtue that is called *religio*, as has been written above, and they all have one source in the name, just as all manner of religious persons originally come forth out of one virtue; and on that account *religio* is an analogical noun. *Et sic est finis hujus* \[And so this is the end of it\].
