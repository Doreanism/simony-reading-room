# Title II. On Simony

Having completed the treatise on hypocrisy, we must now turn to simony, which likewise constitutes an injury to the grace of the Holy Spirit. Concerning it, the following must be considered: first, the name; second, what it is; third, in what ways it is contracted; fourth, its material distinctions; fifth, the penalty of simony; sixth, the cases in which it may be doubted whether simony is committed; seventh, satisfaction for it.
