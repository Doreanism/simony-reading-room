To the contrary. a. To preach is a spiritual thing; but a spiritual thing must not be set under a temporal price; therefore it is not licit to preach for a temporal price; therefore in such a case simony is committed.

b. Likewise, Bede: “Those who exercise the spiritual grace which they have received in the Church by the Lord’s bestowal, not with simple intention but for the sake of human recompense, make the house of the Lord a house of trafficking.” Such persons therefore commit simony.

#### Problem II. Whether masters of the liberal arts may sell their knowledge.

Next it is asked whether masters of the liberal arts may sell their knowledge.

And it seems that they may. 1. For a spiritual thing is that by which the Holy Spirit is possessed, or signified to be possessed, or given; but by the liberal arts neither is the Holy Spirit possessed, nor signified to be possessed, nor given; therefore such forms of knowledge are not spiritual things, nor things annexed to spiritual things; therefore they can be sold.

2. Likewise, in Proverbs 23:23 it is said: *Buy truth.* But what can freely be bought, can also be sold; therefore such forms of knowledge of the truth can be bought and sold.

To the contrary. a. Knowledge is a gift of God, which is evident from what is said in Exodus 31:6, on the words *God placed \[wisdom\] in his heart*, where it is said that knowledge is a gift of God, as is found in the Interlinear Gloss; but it is not licit to sell the gifts of God, for of these is said that saying of Matthew 10:8: *Freely you have received, freely give*; therefore it is not licit to sell knowledge.

b. Furthermore, it is said in Proverbs 23:23: *Do not sell wisdom and instruction and understanding.* Therefore from the very text of Proverbs it is established that things of this kind do not fall under sale.
