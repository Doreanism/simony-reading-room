### Article IV. On the comparison of simony with idolatry.

The further question is raised about the comparison of simony with idolatry.

For it seems that simony, considered as a sale, is punished more severely than idolatry. 1. The sin whose punishment is greater is shown to be the greater sin. “For who doubts that what is more severely avenged has been more wickedly committed?” — as is found in Causa XXIV, q. 1, *Non afferamus*. That the punishment is more severe is shown by Ambrosius in his book *De mysteriis*, where it is said: “Whereas those who sinned against God by the error of idolatry are seen to be punished only to the fourth generation — as is found in Deuteronomy, where the Moabites and the rest shall not enter the Church of God until the third and fourth generation — the sentence by which the seed of Gehazi is condemned forever, by prophetic authority, for the desire of possessing seems clearly more severe.” For it is said: *Leprosy shall cleave to thee and to thy seed for ever*. From this it appears that a more severe punishment is inflicted for the sin of simony than for the sin of idolatry, and so it seems to be the greater sin.
