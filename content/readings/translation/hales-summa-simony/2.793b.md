But the simoniac does not act in this way; rather, the simoniac acts by selling the grace of the Holy Spirit in the sacraments as if the Holy Spirit were subject to him in his operation. And this is what is said: “Every master sells, if he wishes, what he has, whether a servant or anything else of his possessions.”

The Moabites, moreover, shall not enter the Church of God even after the tenth generation, forever. From this it is gathered that, in this respect, an equal punishment was inflicted for each sin; nevertheless the sin of idolatry is the greater, and a graver punishment is owed to it.

As for what is said — that he shall not enter the Church of God until the third and fourth generation, as found in the other translation — this can be referred to the mystical sense.

That which is said, *Leprosy shall cleave to thee and to thy seed for ever*, can also be understood spiritually of those who imitate that kind of sin. Hence Ambrosius: “When the seed of Gehazi is condemned forever, what is this but that you should understand the seed of vices rather than the seed of lineage?”

Furthermore, as for what is said of the Moabites — that they shall not enter the Church of the Lord — a reason is subjoined: *Because they would not meet you with bread and water when you came forth out of Egypt, and because they hired against thee Balaam, the son of Beor, that he should curse thee*. And so a different cause is given than idolatry, although they themselves were idolaters. As for the letter, such a punishment was established “so that, even when they had been made proselytes, they should not be received into the Church of God until the tenth generation.”
