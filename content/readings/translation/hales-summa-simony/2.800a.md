#### Problem III. Whether it is lawful to give money for the celebration of an anniversary or for the absolution of the excommunicated.

Then the question is asked, I. whether it is lawful to give fixed revenues and to bargain for the celebration of an anniversary.

II. Likewise, the question is raised concerning those who receive money from the excommunicated before absolving them.

And it seems that they commit simony. 1. For they place under a price what is beyond price.

But the contrary seems to be the case, a. from the custom of the Church and from the fact that pecuniary penalties have been established for the correction of offenders.

b. Moreover, it is just that where there is a burden, there should also be a recompense; but it is a burden for the church to be obliged in perpetuity to celebrate an anniversary; it is therefore fitting that a fixed recompense be assigned.

#### Problem IV. Whether it is lawful to give temporal goods to religious men so that they may pray for one.

Then the question is asked whether it is lawful to give temporal goods to religious men so that they may pray for one.

And it seems that it is not. 1. For prayer is a spiritual thing: in it the Holy Spirit is sought, that is, the gift of the Holy Spirit; but it is not lawful to place under a price what is beyond price; therefore it is not lawful to give temporal goods so that prayers be made.
