6. Similarly, it can be asked what is meant by “attached to a spiritual thing”. For in a case of necessity consecrated things, such as crosses, chalices, and the like, can be sold, and yet these are attached \[to spiritual things\]; therefore to sell what is attached to a spiritual thing is not simony.

\[Solution\]: 1. To this it must be said that the sin of simony is committed by the deliberate willing itself. But it is one thing to speak of simony insofar as someone is excommunicated from the Church on its account, and another insofar as it is sin in general; and by “excommunicated” I mean deprived of the communion of the faithful. For if one speaks of simony insofar as excommunication is inflicted — which is the deprivation of the communion of the faithful — simony as deliberate willing does not suffice, but a manifest act proceeding from it is required. To this effect Urban says: “We are speaking of manifest matters; but God is the knower and judge of hidden things.” And he is speaking there of excommunication. — But if excommunication is taken as deprivation of the effect of the sacraments, then on this account anyone excommunicate or suspended is said to be by the law itself a simoniac, though he is to be excommunicated by the Church when this comes to its notice. Ambrose: “Whoever henceforth shall be found to have offered any reward whatsoever for obtaining the dignity of a divine gift, let him know that from that time he is condemned under the reproach of anathema and excluded from participation in the body and blood of the Lord.” But as Gregory Nazianzen says, deliberate willing is taken when there is already an application to the act. — As for the objection from the analogy with usury, it must be said that the passage there speaks of simony insofar as it is sin, not insofar as it renders one irregular in the judgment of the Church.

2. To the objection concerning the addition of the qualifier “deliberate”, it must be said that, although that qualifier is not added, it is nonetheless sin. But this qualifier “deliberate” is added to aggravate the crime.
