\[Solution\]: I. To this it is answered, and first to what is said concerning the blessing of those being married, that it is not lawful to exact and extort «money» beforehand «for the blessing of those being married and the like», as is held in *Extra, De simonia, Ad apostolicam audientiam*. If, however, there is a pious custom in certain regions that some things be offered from «the devotion of the faithful», it is fitting «that the sacraments of the Church be conferred freely; but those who maliciously strive to alter a laudable custom should be restrained by the bishop of the place, once the truth is known».

\[To the objections\]: 1-a. As for what is objected, that

#### Problem II. Whether a priest may sing Mass for money.

The question is then asked whether a priest may sing Mass for money.

And it seems that he may not. 1. For it is said upon that text of Hosea 5:1: *Judgment is against you, priests, since you have become a snare to the watchtower and a net spread upon Mount Tabor*, where Bernardus expounds this of priests who spread a net of cupidity over the sacrament of the altar.

2. Moreover, as is held in John 2:15, the Lord cast out of the temple those who were buying and selling. But who are more buyers and sellers than those who expose the Sacrament of the Eucharist for sale?

3. Moreover, in Micah 3:11 the priests are rebuked, *who answered for a reward*, according to another reading, where we have *who taught for a reward*; but what is it to answer for a reward, if not to celebrate for money?
