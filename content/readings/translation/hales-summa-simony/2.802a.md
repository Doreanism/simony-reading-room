Secondly, inquiry is made concerning the simony which consists in selling what is annexed to a spiritual thing.

I. And first concerning the right of receiving tithes. Concerning which it is asked whether it is lawful to sell the right of receiving tithes, or whether simony is committed.

And it seems that simony is not committed. 1. For knights have the right of receiving the *particampum*, and that right is called terrestrial. Now the *particampum* is the tenth or eleventh or twelfth part of the sheaves according to the diverse customs of diverse regions. Knights, therefore, can have the right of receiving tithes; therefore it is not simony, since no spiritual thing is set under a price here.

To the contrary. a. The right of receiving tithes is annexed to a spiritual thing, namely to Holy Orders: for the clergy alone have the right of receiving tithes, as is held from the Lateran Council: «We forbid that laymen, holding back tithes to the peril of their own souls, should be able to transfer them to other laymen». But simony is the will to buy or sell a spiritual thing or what is annexed to a spiritual thing; therefore it is simoniacal to sell or to buy the right of receiving tithes.
