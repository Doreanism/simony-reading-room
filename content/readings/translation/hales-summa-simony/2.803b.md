For the right of receiving tithes is not annexed to a village or to any portion of land, but is annexed to the clerical Order; hence, just as the Order cannot be sold, so neither, consequently, can the right of receiving tithes.

II. 1. To what is asked about the tithes themselves, it must be said that “the fruits themselves can be sold or bought, or even given to laymen for cause, but the right of tithe in no way.” But what Gregorius says is to be understood of the right of tithe, not of the fruits themselves: for the fruits can be granted for cause to knights themselves by the Church on the authority of a prelate, for the defense of the Church or in favor of Christendom, just as they were granted to certain knights in the kingdom of Aragon, namely that they should receive the tithes of all places which they could acquire by expelling pagans. But if this is so, they do not receive them in perpetuity by common law, but by privilege and by the Church’s concession, as long as the cause endures. For if afterward the Church should be enormously burdened, or they should not aid the Church but assail it, or abuse the privilege, they would not lawfully receive those tithes.

III. To what is asked about the offerings, it must be said that laymen neither have the right of receiving the offerings of the church, nor does it belong to them to receive those which are made at the altar; nevertheless these can be given to them for cause, either for the repair of the church or for some honorable use, provided however that no right be claimed.

IV. 2. To the last point it must be said that, according to what is stated in the Decretal, tithes cannot pass into a lay fief. For no right of ownership has been granted to laymen by the Church, but a certain right of receiving fruits for a time for the protection of the Church, and not in the name of inheritance. That in some regions they do pass to heirs, where the cause does not exist, this is by the Church’s permission on account of the multitude of offenders, not that thereby any right is given to them.
