\[Solution\]: I. To this it is answered that it is not lawful, when a pact intervenes, to accept revenues for the celebration of an anniversary. If, however, the devotion of the faithful is expressed in conferring a revenue, the church is bound, on account of the devotion of these same faithful, to celebrate for those for whom it is asked.

II. To the second it must be said that money is not to be received or given for the benefit of absolution, as is held in *Extra*, *De simonia*, *Ad aures*. If, however, certain penalties have been established by the Church for the correction of offenders, they may consequently be exacted, not for the benefit of absolution itself, but so that, on account of the penalty which he fears, the offender may thereafter guard himself against the offense. It must be seen to, however, that no more be ascribed to cupidity than to correction: for then it could be a sin.
