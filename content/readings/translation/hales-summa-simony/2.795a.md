#### Problem III. Whether it is licit to give money for the redemption of one’s own right.

Next it is asked whether it is licit to give money for the redemption of one’s own right.

And it seems so. a. For it is licit for anyone to redeem his own right, and it is said in the *Extra*, *De simonia*, *Dilectus*, that restitution is to be made to him who has redeemed his harassment, because the one who took the money committed simony; from which it follows that it is licit to redeem; therefore simony is not committed if money is given to redeem a harassment which is unjustly inflicted upon him concerning a spiritual right.

On the contrary. 1. It can happen that the one from whom it is sought has some right in the very thing that is sought; but that right is spiritual; it is not, however, licit to buy a spiritual thing, just as it is not licit to sell one; therefore it is not licit to redeem it.

#### Problem IV. Whether it is licit to exact something for spiritual works that are owed.

Further it is also asked whether it is licit to exact something for spiritual works that are owed, such as for a consecration or visitation.
