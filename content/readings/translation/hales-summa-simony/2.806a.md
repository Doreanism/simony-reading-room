#### Problem VI. Whether the right of patronage can be sold together with an estate.

Next it is asked whether it can be sold together with an estate.

And it seems that it cannot. a. For if it is sold together with the estate, the whole is sold; therefore so is each part of that whole; but the patronage is a part of that whole; therefore the right of patronage can be sold; if, then, “the right of patronage cannot be sold by itself,” it cannot be sold as part of the whole either.

b. Likewise, it is not lawful to sell a prebend and a town together; therefore neither is it lawful to sell the right of patronage and a town together.

c. Likewise, the right of patronage is beyond price in itself, and much more so when included in a whole estate; therefore, just as it ought not to be sold in itself, neither ought it to be sold as part of a whole.
