## Chapter IV. On the material differences of simony.

Fourthly, the question is asked concerning the material differences of simony, which are considered according to the difference between the spiritual itself and what is annexed to spiritual things. There are three differences:

### Article I. On the spiritual thing itself.

#### Problem I. Whether it is lawful to give money for the matrimonial blessing.

Concerning the first thus: I. The question is asked whether it is lawful for anyone to give money for the matrimonial blessing, if the priest is unwilling to perform it without a price, the marriage already existing.

And it seems that it is, 1. because it is lawful to redeem one’s own right; but it is the right of those joined by marriage that the blessing be given them; therefore it is lawful to give for obtaining the blessing, if it cannot be done otherwise.

To the contrary. a. The blessing is a spiritual thing; and it is not lawful to give money for a spiritual thing; therefore it is not lawful to give for it. What, then, is to be done in such a case?
