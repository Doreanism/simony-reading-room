### Article II. On the gift from service.

#### Problem I. Whether it is permitted by means of service to obtain a spiritual benefice.

Secondly, the question is asked concerning the gift from service: whether it is permitted by means of service to obtain a spiritual benefice.

And it seems that it is. a. For Gregory says: “Those who serve the interests of the Church deserve to enjoy ecclesiastical remuneration, so that those who voluntarily submit themselves to the duties of service may worthily be consoled by our provisions.” From which it is held that by means of service someone can obtain an ecclesiastical benefice.

b. Likewise, Gregory: “It is just that those should receive a stipend who are found to lend their service for a time.”

c. Furthermore, if ecclesiastical benefices are to be conferred, on whom are they more fittingly to be conferred than on those who serve the Church? Therefore it is permitted to confer an ecclesiastical benefice in view of service; and by the same reasoning, to serve as well — and here bodily service is understood.
