## Chapter II. What simony is

### Article I. On the definition of simony

Secondly, it is asked what simony is. It is defined thus: Simony is a deliberate will to buy or to sell something spiritual or something annexed to the spiritual. This account may be drawn from what Gregorius Nazianzenus says: “Whoever strives to purchase the gift of God for a price, there is no doubt that he can in no way thereafter remain in or be restored to sacred Orders. For what else is it to procure the gift of the Holy Spirit for a price than a capital crime and simoniacal heresy?” From this it is gathered that simony is contracted in the deliberate will to buy or to sell, etc.
