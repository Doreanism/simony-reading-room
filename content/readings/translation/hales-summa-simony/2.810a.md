\[Solution\]: To this it is answered by a distinction that, if money has been received from someone holding a benefice in a church, it must be restored to that church to whose injury it was given. But if someone has given money somewhere in order to be received into some monastery, and that person is transferred from that monastery to another, the money must be restored to the monastery to which he is transferred, so that, just as it has the burden, so it may have the benefit.
