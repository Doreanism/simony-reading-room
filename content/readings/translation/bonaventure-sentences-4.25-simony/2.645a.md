For they administer the sacraments, but they do not obtain the fruit of the sacraments, since the Holy Spirit is not outside the catholic Church. And therefore, in all those sacraments whose truth is distinct from their utility, it is universally true that they can exist among heretics. Now all the sacraments are of this kind except the sacrament of penance, whose reality is interior contrition, without which no absolution is true, and with which no absolution is empty or useless. Hence they can administer all the sacraments, and do truly administer them, except for the absolution of penance alone; and therefore none are repeated except that one — unless perhaps extreme unction. And so, since order possesses a character which exists apart from the grace of the Holy Spirit by which sins are blotted out, it must be said that among them \[heretics\] order can be truly administered.

## QUAESTIO III

Whether a simoniac bishop can sell any order.

Thirdly, it is asked whether a simoniac bishop is able to sell any order. And that he can, the following appears:

1. Because nothing is forbidden unless its being done is possible — otherwise the prohibition would be in vain. But the selling of a holy order is forbidden: in Causa prima, quaestione prima, Calixtus forbids it: “Following the examples of the holy Fathers, we forbid by every means, by the authority of the Apostolic See, that anyone in the Church of God be ordained or promoted through money.” Therefore, etc.
