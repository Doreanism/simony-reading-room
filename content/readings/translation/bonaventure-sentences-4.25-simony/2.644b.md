— text: “It is certain that he who has lost the honor cannot give the honor”; but such is the heretic: therefore, etc.

2. Likewise, *Causa* I, *quaestio* I: “The blessing is turned into a curse for him who is ordained to the end that he may become a heretic”: therefore, if no one is promoted to orders through a curse, the conclusion is clear, etc.

3. Likewise, to promote someone to orders is something greater than to absolve or to bind; but a heretic who has been cut off can absolve or bind no one: therefore he can promote no one to holy orders.

4. Likewise, a lower power cannot act except through continuity with the higher power and through its own power; but the fullness of power is in the Supreme Pontiff, from whom the heretic who has been cut off is separated and disjoined: therefore it seems that he can give nothing at all of those things which pertain to the ecclesiastical powers.

### Conclusio

Among heretics orders can be dispensed as to their truth, but not as to their usefulness.
