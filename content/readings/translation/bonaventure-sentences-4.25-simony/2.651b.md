6. Likewise, continence is of necessity annexed to sacred orders; but no one is bound to continence unless he has the use of reason and consents through his own will: therefore at least to the sacred orders no one can be promoted before the years of discretion: therefore the use of reason is necessary.

ON THE CONTRARY (*Sed contra*): 1. Other Sacraments which imprint a character, such as baptism and confirmation, can be given before the use of reason: therefore by parity of reasoning it seems that this Sacrament also can.

2. Likewise, this very thing seems clear from the *Extra*, *De Clerico ordinato per saltum*, where it is said that someone was promoted to the minor orders before the years of discretion, and afterwards, without iteration, was permitted to minister in such offices.

3. Likewise, when a priest is ordained, the keys of binding and loosing are given to him, even if the priest lacks knowledge: therefore much more can the order be given to a boy, even if he lacks the use of reason.
