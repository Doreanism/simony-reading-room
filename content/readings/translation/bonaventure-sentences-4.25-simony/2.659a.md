Under carnal love is reckoned another member, namely the flesh, which embraces disordered love whether with regard to kinsmen or with regard to others, as when someone, out of love for a harlot, promotes the son of a harlot. But if it is a matter of pride, then in two ways: either insofar as he seeks favor, like the hypocrite, and so it is popular favor or fame; or insofar as he seeks to be praised, and so it is the tongue.
