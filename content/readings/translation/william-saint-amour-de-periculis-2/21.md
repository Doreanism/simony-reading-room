# Through whom, or what manner of men, the dangers of the last times will come. CHAPTER II.

Concerning the second point, namely, through whom these dangers will press upon us, the Apostle foretells in 2 Timothy 3, saying that there shall be in the Church certain men, *lovers of themselves*, through whom the said dangers will press in. Now those are said to love themselves who, although they are willing to correct others, nevertheless are unwilling to be corrected by other men in their own deeds, even when those deeds are sometimes perverse. Hence such men love themselves more than the truth, and therefore more than God, who is the truth, John 14. For as Gregorius says in the *Pastoral*: *He who wills to do perverse things and wills the rest to be silent about them is himself a witness to himself that he wills himself to be loved more than the truth, in that he is unwilling for the truth not to be defended against him.* Likewise, he loves himself more than God who seeks his own honor more than the honor of God; whence Augustinus in *De vita Christiana*: *Those love God who pursue nothing other than that whereby the name of God may be glorified.* Therefore those, especially who are in the state of perfection, and yet who seek and pursue their own temporal honor,
