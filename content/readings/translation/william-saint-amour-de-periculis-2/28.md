Therefore, if regulars meddle in preaching, it appears that they are not sent by God, and so are to be reckoned as pseudo-preachers. Thus, then, it is clear from what has been said who *the penetrators of houses* are and who *the pseudo-preachers* are; and it is clear, too, that through such men the dangers of the last times will press upon, or are pressing upon, the universal Church.
