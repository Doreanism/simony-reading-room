\[house\]-penetrators, the Gloss expounds literally as follows. *They penetrate houses who enter the houses of those whose government of souls does not pertain to them, and pry into their private affairs* — that is, into their secrets. This cannot be done unless they thrust themselves forward to hear their confessions; for this is to penetrate the houses of the aforesaid men, namely, to pry into their consciences, which are called houses, in Ecclesiasticus 32: *Run home first to thy house*, Gloss: *that is, into the house of conscience*. Into this house the Rector of souls — who holds the cure committed to him by the Church — enters through the door, and to him it is commanded in Proverbs 27, *Be diligent to know the countenance of thy cattle*. Gloss: *To the Pastor of the Church it is said, diligently apply care to those over whom thou hast been set; recognize the dispositions and acts of each one; and if thou findest any vice in them, remember to chastise it speedily*. Hence to the Pastor the door of the house ought to be opened through confession, according to that saying of Boethius in *The Consolation*, *if thou awaitest healing, thou must uncover thy wound*. But a stranger, who does not hold the cure, ought not to be received into this house. Ecclesiasticus 11: *Bring not every man into thy house, for many are the snares of the deceitful*. And in the same place: *Receive a stranger in, and he shall overthrow thee with a whirlwind, and shall estrange thee from thy own*. And therefore concerning such a stranger the Apostle says, Romans 14: *Who art thou that judgest another man’s servant? To his own lord he standeth or falleth*. A stranger, then, if he pries into the conscience of another’s subject, does not enter through the door, but climbs up some other way, and therefore is said to penetrate the house as a thief and a robber. John 10: *He that entereth not by the door into the sheepfold of the sheep, but climbeth up some other way, the same is a thief and a robber*. Thus, then, we have who they are that penetrate houses.

Next the Apostle says in the same chapter, 2 Timothy 3, *And lead captive silly women loaded with sins*. Gloss, literally: *With crafty and cunning words they seduce women, and through them their husbands, just as the Devil first seduced Eve and through her Adam. And in the same manner they seduce gullible men after the fashion of women* — that is, *to themselves, or draw them to their own counsels*; for so this verb *seduce* is expounded in Revelation 19, *Satan shall be loosed, and shall seduce the nations*. Gloss: *that is, shall draw them away from Christ to himself*. In like manner, these men also will seduce both gullible women and men — that is, they will draw them from the counsel of their Prelates to their own counsels, and at last will lead them captive — that is, will reduce them under their own power, binding them, namely, to live according to their counsels: for to be led captive is to be brought from one’s own power, or from the power of him to whom one is subject, into the power of another.
