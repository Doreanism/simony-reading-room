Now out of this perverse self-love, as out of an evil root, those men, *lovers of themselves*, will fall into various kinds of sins, so that some of them will become *covetous* of money or of worldly glory; others will become *haughty* against God or against the Lord, namely, because they will be unwilling to be under prelates, but rather to be set over them; others will become *proud* of the honors bestowed upon them; others will become *blasphemers* of God through heresies; others will become *disobedient to parents*, that is, to the prelates of the Church; others will become *ungrateful*, for the goods bestowed upon them; others will become *wicked*, in perpetrating grave sins, who pass beyond the measure of sinning and kill their own souls or the souls of others, as those who repay evil for good to those who correct them; others will become *without affection*, having no compassion for the infirm, namely sinners, but rather disdaining them; others will become *slanderers*, that is, laying crimes upon others, or detractors, namely those who endeavor to stain the reputation of the saints; others will become *incontinent*, not restraining the evil cravings of gluttony or lust; others will become *without kindness*, not knowing how to come to the aid of others; others will become *traitors*, namely of another’s secrets, while revealing what they knew should be concealed; others will become *insolent* or impudent, that is, treating or handling shamelessly the things which ought to be treated with modesty, namely concerning other men; others will become *blind*, that is, not understanding what they affirm, nor the matters with which they deal; others will become *swollen*, namely with an inflated heart; others will become *lovers of pleasures more than of God*, that is, preferring carnal delights to spiritual ones. And this the Apostle says in these words: *And men shall be lovers of themselves.* Gloss: *And* stands for *because*, as if he were saying: For this reason perilous times shall press upon us, because there will be in the Church men who are *lovers of themselves, covetous, haughty, blasphemers, ungrateful, wicked, without affection, without peace, slanderers, incontinent, without kindness, traitors, insolent or impudent, swollen, lovers of pleasures more than of God.*

Now from all these kinds of sinners, some will come together into that sect through which perilous times will press upon us. Whence in 2 Timothy 3, after those words, *And men shall be lovers of themselves, covetous, haughty,* etc., the Apostle adds, *Of these are they who penetrate houses* — that is, from these kinds of sinners some will come together into the sect of those who penetrate houses, through whom, as house-penetrators, those dangers will press in, as will become clear.
