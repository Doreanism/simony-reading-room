# On Simony

Simony is so named from Simon Magus, who tried to buy with money the power of giving the Holy Spirit so that he might afterwards profit by reselling the signs, as is plain from Acts 8 and from *j. q. j. saluator*. By contrast, “Gehazianism” is not so called from Gehazi, who merely resold a gift of God. On this topic see Thomas throughout the tract in II-II, q. 100, and in IV, dist. xxv, and in Quodlibet II, and likewise in VII.
