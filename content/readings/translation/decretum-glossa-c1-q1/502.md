## C. LXXXI. Against the Holy Spirit a word is said when from dispersion one never returns

> Thus a word a is uttered against the Holy Spirit, when from dispersion one never c comes to the congregation b, which received the Holy Spirit for remitting sins. To which congregation, even if through an evil cleric, but nevertheless a Catholic minister, reprobate and feigned, anyone with an unfeigned heart should approach; in him the Holy Spirit works the remission of sins; who in the holy church works in such a way that he flees the reprobate, and yet d through their ministry gathers the upright.

*¶Holy Spirit. In this place the opinion of blessed Augustine is briefly referred to; more fully, however, above same c. spiritus sanctus.*

*The same in the sermon on the remission of sins.*

## C. LXXXII. Sins are remitted not by the merits of men, but by the power of the Holy Spirit

> So that the Lord might evidently show this — that by the Holy Spirit, which he gave to his faithful, sins are forgiven, not by the merits of men — in a certain place e, when rising from the dead, he thus said: [Receive the Holy Spirit;] and immediately f added, [If you remit g anyone’s sins, they are remitted to him,] that is, the Spirit h remits, not you. Therefore γ, just as he whom the priest tolerates in the church is nevertheless outside the church, and outside the body, whose head is Christ, if the doorkeeper i has not opened to him δ: so it is not on that account [that] he is outside the church, and is not of the body, if anyone is compelled by the prejudice k of the shepherd to be outside.

*¶Proinde. The very words of this part have not been found in blessed Augustine. Most similar ones are had in Origen, Homily 14 on c. 24 of Leviticus; they are reported below 24. q. 3. c. cum aliquis. Si quis non recto.*

*¶Has opened. In some ancient codices it reads: opened. In others, may open. From both, however, the negation is absent.*

*Likewise Ambrose, on those who are initiated into the mysteries, c. 5.*

## C. LXXXIII. The Lord considers not the merits of persons, but the offices of priests

> When the scri-

**¶When the scripture. CASE.** Who doubts that the Holy Spirit is present in the sacraments of priests, when in the Old Testament fire is read to have descended from heaven at the prayers both of good and of evil men? — each of which is proved by the history of Jeroboam, who was evil, etc.

¶At the end a man is destroyed; for with virtues removed the soul is [removed]. *15. q. 4. l. 1.* and *11. q. 3. si episcopus forte, supra ea. q. remissionem, & infra 11. q. 3. quid obest.* H.

**¶Thus a word is. CASE.** It is said that the heretic then sins against the Holy Spirit, when he disdains to return from schism to the unity and congregation of the church: in which church the Holy Spirit works through a Catholic, even though feigned and reprobate, the remission of sins: and the evil are gathered through such a one.

**a. ¶Thus a word is.** That is, blasphemy. *J. de poe. dist. 1. §. potest. & dist. 4. §. siue.* H.

**b. ¶Congregation.** That is, the unity of the church.

**c. ¶Never.** Thus a sin against the Holy Spirit is only final impenitence: of which Augustine speaks. *de poen. dist. 7. c. 1. & si.* And I have noted *25. dist. qualis.*

**d. ¶And yet.** He says this: because from this one I can also receive [the sacrament] even without necessity. *re. ead. spiritus sanctus.*

**¶So that evidently. CASE.** It is shown in this chapter that not man, but the Holy Spirit remits sins: and this is proved by the authority of the Lord saying; If you remit anyone’s sins, etc. and therefore it happens that the one whom the priest tolerates is also outside the church, if Christ does not open to him: and the unjustly excommunicated is within. Archdeacon.

**e. ¶In a certain place.** Thus also an advocate can allege, as a certain law says *45. dist. cum beatus.* ADDITION. To the same point *ca. multae. 45. dist. §. hinc etiam.* and *32. q. 7. quaedam.* Archdeacon.

**f. ¶And immediately.** Argument that the order of words is to be considered, as *7. dist. arg. exemplo.* and that something of the words must be weighed. *infrà 24. q. 1. alienus.*

**g. ¶You remit.** Well did he say, “you remit,” not “to whom you have judged.” *Vt de poe. dist. 1. quem poenitet.* arg. *infra 1. q. 3. illud. & c. 30. distin. si nemo contemnat. infra ea. q. dictum est. j. de consec. dist. 4. nemo.* H.

**h. ¶That is, the Spirit.** Christ. The same is *J. ca. q. ordinationes.*

**i. ¶Doorkeeper.** That is, Christ. The same is the door, as *J. ca. q. ordinationes.*

**k. ¶Prejudice.** That is, depraved judgment. *Prejudice,* that is, by depraved judgment. *J. 24. d. 3. si quis.*
