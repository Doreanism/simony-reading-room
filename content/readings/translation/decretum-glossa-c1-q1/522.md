## C. CXII. Those who return from heresy to the Church should, in whatever order they are found, persevere by indulgence

> Every cleric of any order whatsoever, who, abandoning catholic unity, has stained himself by the contagion of heretical or schismatic association, if he returns to the Church with legitimate satisfaction and condemnation of his errors, let him have this indulgence: that, with every hope of promotion taken from him, he may remain in the order in which he is found.

*Concerning those, however, who are simoniacally ordained by non-simoniacs (as for example those who, by giving money to the archdeacon or to the bishop’s counselors — the bishop being ignorant — bring it about that they receive holy orders from his hand), Gregory VII writes thus in the Roman synod held in the year of the Lord 1078, November 19, c. 5.*

**α. ¶Januarius.** It is also cited thus *infra ead. q. 7. c. saluberrimum.* And so it is found in the manuscript codices of Leo, although in the printed editions it is “Julian.” Moreover the very words of the epistle are quoted in the said chapter *Saluberrimum*, and in part *supra ead. c. si quis haeretica.* For here the opinion is rather asserted.

**a. ¶Authentic.** That is, fruitful: unless he has renounced the execution of the thing obtained, as *infra ea. q. si quis neque.*

**b. ¶Has been practiced \[Usurpatum est\].** That is, has been done against strict rigor: argument *C. de iur. do. ubi.*

**c. ¶Novatianists.** *Infra ea. quaest. 7. Maximum. & c. Donatum.*

**d. ¶Lesser \[heretics\].** For with the greater heretics no dispensation is made, as *infra q. 7. c. 1.*

**e. ¶So also.** With simoniacs a dispensation is to be made, as will now be held.

**f. ¶In other respects.** That is to say: and this contributes to dispensation.

**g. ¶Let them be acknowledged.** Argument that if in a document there is something false in some part, and in some part it is true, we ought to receive it. *C. de transf. si ex falsis.* Argument to the contrary *9. dist. si ad sacras. & 42. dist. cap. quiescamus.*

**h. ¶Every \[Omnis\].** \[¶Let him have.\] If he was not stained by ratified anointing, as *7. q. 7. saluberrimum.*

**i. ¶We find.** If he has received those orders simoniacally, and \[as to\] with whom a dispensation is made and with whom not, these verses say:

> If an order is given twice, or baptism repeated,

> Or if it is preferred that Simon should perform either,
