## C. CXVIII. On the same

> Let no one therefore be permitted to approach to the episcopal honor through ambition. For when this excess is blamed in lay conversation, who doubts but that it inflicts disgrace upon religious persons and those serving God?

*Likewise Gregory to bishop Adeodatus, bk. 2, indict. 11, epist. 48.*

*¶On the same.*

## C. CXIX. On the same

> Therefore be especially solicitous in ordination, and admit by no means to holy orders any but those advanced in age and clean in work; lest perhaps they cease to be forever what they hasten to be prematurely. For of those who are to be placed in holy orders, first examine their life and morals: and that you may be able to bring worthy persons to this office, let no power or supplication of any persons creep up on us. Above all you must be cautious, that no venality may arise in ordination: lest (which God forbid) greater peril threaten both the ordained and the ordainers. Whenever therefore it is necessary to deal with these matters, take grave and experienced men as participants in your counsels, and weigh this with them by common deliberation.

*¶This chapter has been enriched from the original in two places, where it seemed expedient.*

*The same to bishop Columbus, bk. 2, indict. 11, epist. 47.*

*¶Let no one be promoted to holy orders by favor or venality.*
