Yet were the divine sacraments which were among the Jews, on that account, such b as they themselves were? — these sacraments c which the Lord himself also received, and to celebrate which he sent the lepers whom he had cleansed: and the angel e stood by Zacharias d as he was ministering, and announced that he was heard in the temple. ## C. XXXVIII. On the same

> But many† are the operations g of the Holy Spirit, which the Apostle, when in a certain passage h he had recalled \[them\] in such measure as he judged sufficient, thus concluded: \[All these things, &c. one and the same Spirit works, dividing to each as he wills.\] Since therefore the sacrament is one thing, which even Simon Magus was able to have: prophecy† is another, which is wont to be wrought even in wicked men, as in Saul i; the operation† of the Spirit is another, which only the good k can have, as is “the end of the commandment, love from a pure heart, and a good conscience, and unfeigned faith”; whatever heretics and schismatics receive, charity, which covers a multitude of sins, is the proper gift of catholic unity and peace: nor is it in all, because not all belong to it.

## C. XXXIX. On the same

> The remission† of sins

*† al. remissam] orig.*

**a. ¶Justified.** That is, less wicked. *arg. 8. q. 1. uercor*\[?\]. Hence say, “you have justified,” by comparison with a better life, not by the power of judgment.

**b. ¶Such.** That is, the sacrifice is such as the one sacrificing is. *J. ea. Dominus declaravit. ver. item nos dicimus.* ADDITION. As if to say no, below in the same place, *Dominus*. Huguccio.

**c. ¶Which.** Namely the sacraments, as circumcision, when he was presented with the offerings in the temple. *De consecratione, dist. 4. proprie.* In the end.

**d. ¶And by Zacharias.** Behold, through the good, he shows that there was sacrifice among the Jews.

**e. ¶The angel.** History. It is related in the New Testament that there was in the days of Herod a priest by the name of Zacharias, and his wife by the name of Elizabeth. Now David, wishing to enlarge the worship of the Lord, instituted 24 high priests, of whom however one was greater, who was called the prince of priests; of which number one was Zacharias, who when on the day of propitiation he was placing the incense, an angel foretold to him a son to be born of his wife, on account of which he was struck mute until the day of birth, because he had refused to believe.

**f. ¶And correct.** Argument: whether on account of detesting the persons one is to detest his sayings. Above *27. dist. v\[ers\]. si quid ueri. & c. turbat.* & below *23. q. 4. quid ergo. J. ea. q. 1. Dominus declaravit. J. 19. dist. sm*\[?\]. *J. q. 2. si de area.* To the contrary: *9. dist. si ad sacras.* To the contrary: *dist. 16. canones.* Say as in the decretal *si ad sacras*. Or say that the useful is vitiated by the useless, when there is prejudice to another. *65. dist. c. 1.*

**¶But many.** THE CASE. There are many operations of the Holy Spirit, which Augustine does not enumerate here, but he sends us to the Apostle, who enumerating them adds, *All these things, &c.* Of these operations some are in the good and the bad, some only in the good. In the good and the bad: as the sacraments, baptism in Simon, and prophecy in Saul, each of whom was bad. In the good only: as charity and other virtues.

**g. ¶Operations.** That is, many are the gifts, which the Holy Spirit works. *30. q. 1. pervenit. 23. q. 4. Nabuchodonosor. §. de Tyriis.*

**h. ¶In a certain passage.** That is, in the first epistle to the Corinthians, chapter 12.

**i. ¶As in Saul.** And in Caiaphas, *Expedit ut unus moriatur, &c.* Below in the same place, *q. prophetavit.*

**K. ¶The good.** Namely through indwelling grace. *J. 24. q. 3. de illis qui. & ead. cau. q. 1. ubi sana*\[?\]. H. Johannes de Faventiis.

**¶Remission.** THE CASE. He says here that when anyone is baptized by anyone whatsoever, even a wicked person, the remission of sins which he obtains through baptism, the wicked man does not give him, but the merit of the Church. For only to the good was it said, *Whose sins you remit, &c.* Only that dove has peace, and therefore binds and looses. And only that one is loosed who has peace with the dove, that is the Church.
