## C. XXV. What is begun with a bad beginning is not brought to a good ending

> A position of authority which ª either sedition extorted, or ambition seized: even if it does not offend in its conduct and acts ª: nevertheless from the very † beginning is ruinous as an example ᵇ. And it is difficult ᶜ, that those things which have been begun with an evil beginning ᵉ should be brought through to a good outcome ᵈ.

*† al. ipso.*

*From the same Gregory to the bishop of Antioch, book 7, epistle 47, indiction 2.*

## C. XXVI. A good work is not built up where Christ is not the foundation

> Since Paul says: \[No one ᶠ can lay any other foundation than that which has been laid, which is Christ Jesus;\] consequently it is proved, that where Christ is not the foundation, there is no superstructure of a good work.

*¶In blessed Gregory it is read as follows. “But that distinguished preacher says: foundation, &c.”\] Whoever therefore with the love of God and of neighbor holds the firmness of the faith which is in Christ, has placed the same Jesus Christ, the son of God and man, as foundation with himself. It is therefore to be hoped that where Christ is the foundation, the building also of good works will follow.*

*The same, to Syagrius and other bishops, book 7, epistle 110.*

## C. XXVII. Alms cannot be made from money acquired by simony

> It is not to be considered alms, if what is taken from illicit ᵍ things is distributed to the poor; for he who takes wickedly with this intention, that as it were he may distribute well, is rather burdened than helped α. *And a little later.* ¶Hence also it is certain that even if monasteries, or hospices, or anything else, are constructed from the money which is given for sacred orders, it does not profit by way of merit. *And a little later.* ¶Therefore, lest under pretext of alms we strive to receive anything with sin, sacred Scripture openly forbids us, saying: \[The sacrifices of the wicked are abominable, which are offered from crime.\] For whatever in the sacrifice of God is offered from crime, all...
