# Causa 1, Question 1

*(Continuing canon C. XXXII from p. 482.)*

> as† you have handed \[them\] over to the Church.

*† al. eg*

*The same, book 4 on baptism against the Donatists, c. 12.*

## C. XXXIII. On the same

> Neither therefore outside, just as neither within, can anyone who is on the part of the devil†, either in himself or in any other, defile the sacrament which is of Christ.

*The same to Emeritus, epistle 164.*

*¶The sacraments, which are not changed, are proved to be common to catholics and heretics.*

## C. XXXIIII. On the same

> The sacraments, which you have not changed a, as you have them, are approved by us: lest perhaps, when we wish to correct your perversity, we do sacrilegious injury b to those mysteries of Christ, which in your perversity have not been depraved. For neither had Saul depraved the anointing which he had received, to which anointing King David, the pious servant of God, showed so great honor.

*The same to the Donatists.*

## C. XXXV. On the same

> Just as those who feignedly c \[approach baptism\] truly, not feignedly, receive \[the sacrament\], nor is their baptism reproved; so also those things which schismatics or heretics \[have\], if they do not otherwise have \[them\], nor otherwise do \[them\], than the true Church, when they come to us, we do not emend d, but rather approve.

*¶The very words of the first part of this chapter up to \[?\] utur, are not found in blessed Augustine: but the substance is in book 1 on baptism against the Donatists, c. 12. The rest of this chapter is in the same book, c. 13.*

*The same against the letters of Petilian, book 3, c. 9.*

*¶Let not the sacraments of God be fled by anyone administering them.*

## C. XXXVI. On the same

> Let no one flee the sacraments of God either in a good man or in a wicked man e.

*¶In book 3 against the letters of Petilian, whence this chapter seems to have been taken, it is read thus: “Let no one glory either in a good man: let no one flee the goods of God even in a bad man.” This chapter is referred to above at dist. 32. c. praeter §. verum principia.*

*The same on baptism against the Donatists, book 6, c. 44.*

*¶The malice of men does not profane the sacraments.*

## C. XXXVII. On the same

> Were not f the Sodomites ethnics — that is, gentiles? The Jews therefore were worse g; to whom the Lord says: \[It shall be more tol-

**b. ¶Injury.** Argument that on account of another, injury ought not to be done to another. *De consecratione*, dist. 4. *ostenditur*. ff. *de reg. iur.* *non debet*. Johannes.

**Just as**

**c. ¶Feignedly.** But is baptism to be given to him who approaches feignedly? Say that\* yes, but afterwards he is to be compelled to observe the faith. arg. 45. dist. *de Iudaeis*. Whether however he is to be baptized while ignorant or unwilling or sleeping, you have: extra *de baptismo et eius effectu* cap. *maiores*. §. *item quaeritur*.

*\*See S. Thomas 3.p.q. 68. art. 3. j cor. et ar. 4. ad 2. et 3. et art. 8. et q.69. artic. 9. et 10.*

**d. ¶We do not emend.** Below, *de cons*. dist. 4. *ostenditur*. & c. *solet quaeri*. below in the same place, *si quis inquit*. But to the contrary: J. ea. q. *sicut urgeri*. But here \[it concerns\] the feigning of the receiver, which does not impede: there \[it concerns\] the feigning of the giver, which does impede: or when \[it is\] not in the form of the Church. 32. dist. *nullus*.

**e. ¶Or in a bad man.** That is, tolerated 1, or in necessity: and so this is not contrary to above, dist. 32. *nullus*. & c. *praeter hoc*.

**¶Were not.** THE CASE. Here he shows that among the wicked there were sacraments. For the Jews were worse than the Sodomites: and yet the Lord proved their sacraments to be true, when for the celebration of these he sent the lepers whom he had cleansed: and when his angel stood by Zacharias ministering in the temple, and announced that he had been heard. Nor even did the holy fathers reject the words of the gentiles, where they spoke truth.

**f. ¶Were not.** Augustine wishes to prove in this chapter that among heretics there are true sacraments: which were among the Jews, who were worse than the Sodomites. Also by another reason: because if the gentiles had any good thing, this the holy men did not reject. That however among the Jews there were true sacraments, he proves by the example of the Lord, and of Zacharias: Zacharias however was good: hence from the person there is no proof: but he sacrificed in the same way as the other Jews.

**g. ¶Worse.** J. 24. q. 1. *non afferamus*: because they only transgressed the natural law, but the Jews \[transgressed\] also the written law.
