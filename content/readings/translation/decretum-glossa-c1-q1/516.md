> \[and that\] those ordained by simoniacs through the wicked imposition of the simoniac’s hand have a wounded head. ¶But it is objected a that in the ordination of simoniacs this alone b is found reprehensible — that the grace of the Holy Spirit is supposed to be reduced to a price — but otherwise the form of the sacraments is preserved entire in all respects. Indeed even if penance does not follow, although ordained simoniacally, he is nevertheless truly to be reckoned consecrated c: just as those who formerly were not admitted to the regeneration of sacred baptism or to participation in holy communion except when a price had been given, although \[admitted\] simoniacally, were nonetheless reckoned baptized or partakers of holy communion: but it was only † established by the sacred canons that from then on the sacraments of Christ should not be distributed simoniacally.

## C. XCIX. No prices are to be exacted from those to be baptized or confirmed

> Let priests fix no prices d e for those to be baptized or confirmed among the faithful, nor seek to harass those being reborn f by imposing any kind of contributions: since *what we freely received, we are commanded freely to give*. And therefore let them by no means attempt to exact g h anything from the aforesaid, lest \[the faithful\], whether deterred by the pressure of poverty or driven back by indignation, refuse to approach the causes of their redemption: knowing for certain that those who shall have been detected as having committed forbidden \[acts\], or who have not rather of their own accord corrected what they have committed i, will incur the danger of losing their own office.

*Likewise from the Sixth Synod, ch. 23.*

¶No price ought to be exacted from one receiving the grace † of communion.

## C. C. No price ought to be exacted from one receiving the grace of communion

> Let no bishop or presbyter or deacon k, who dispenses the sacred communion, exact any price l from him who receives the grace α of communion. For neither does the grace seem to be \[grace\], nor do we give the grace of the Holy Spirit for a price; but we grant the worthy to partake of the gift without fraud. But if anyone of those who are numbered in the clergy shall have exacted any price from him to whom he dispenses sacred communion, let him be deposed, as an imitator of simoniacal fraud.
