## C. XLIX. Bodily sacraments which they carry and celebrate, even when separated from the unity of the body of Christ, can present the form of piety

> Bodily sacraments a, which they carry and celebrate even when separated from the unity of the body of Christ, can present the form of piety b: but the invisible and spiritual power of piety cannot be in them; just as feeling does not remain in a man’s limb when it is cut off from the body.

*¶In some old codices this and the following chapter are joined to the preceding.*

*Likewise Ambrose, in the book On Initiating the Untrained.*

¶On the same.

## C. L. The baptism of the perfidious does not heal: it does not cleanse, but pollutes

> The baptism c of the perfidious does not heal; it does not cleanse, but pollutes: because in the flood d of many waters one does not draw near to the Lord; for, as is said by the Apostle: [Whatever is not of faith e is sin.] And it does not profit either those who administer or those for whom it is administered — indeed, it is to their judgment.

> *a. ¶It pollutes. Up to this point Ambrose. But Algerus, book 3 c. 1, in citing this passage, adds [text] up to the word approximatur, and then c. 13, also citing the same book of Blessed Ambrose, adds the rest of this chapter to the end.*

*Likewise Leo, to Nicetas bishop of Aquileia, letter 77 [al. 79] c. 7.*

¶Let those who have received baptism from heretics be confirmed by the laying on of hands.

## C. LI. Those who have received baptism from heretics

> Those who have received baptism from heretics, if they had not been baptized f before, are to be confirmed by the laying on of hands with the sole invocation of the Holy Spirit; because they received only the form of baptism without the power of sanctification. Therefore we preach to all the churches, as you know, that this rule must be kept: that the laver once begun be violated by no repetition, the Apostle saying: [One g Lord, one h faith, one i baptism.] Whose washing is to be profaned by no repetition; but, as we have said, only the sanctification of the Holy Spirit is to be invoked, so that what k no one receives from heretics may be obtained from catholic priests.

> *9. But it must be noted that the heretics — such as the Arians and Sabellians, of whom l some confessed an inequality and others an indistinction of persons m — administered baptism not in the form n of the Church but in [the form] of their own perversity. ¶Concerning these specifically*

**a. ¶Bodily. CASE.** Just as an amputated limb has no feeling, so also bodily baptism [given outside the Church is] without effect.

**a. ¶Bodily.** [Sacraments] which are conferred by the ministry of bodies and around bodies; or because some are bodily, as chrism, or are the forms of bodies, or because they are conferred under bodily species. But do they perish with the body? Argument that they do not. *Infr. ead. quod quidam. 32. q. 7. tantum valet.*

**b. ¶Of piety.** That is, of holy religion and truth: but he says this because piety is there when truth is, &c.

**¶Does not heal. CASE.** It is said in this chapter that the baptism of the perfidious does not profit. And this is proved by the authority of the Apostle, who says: everything that is not of faith is sin; indeed it rather harms those who receive that baptism.

**c. ¶Baptism.** Given outside the form of the Church, as *infr. eod. § sed notandum*.

**d. ¶Who in the flood of waters.** That is, in the multitude of diverse opinions or sects; or in flood only, because baptism alone does not suffice unless grace also be given.

**e. ¶From faith.** That is, with faith working through love.

**¶Those who. CASE.** It is said here that those who are baptized by heretics are to be confirmed by the laying on of the bishop’s hand, so that they may thus receive the grace of the Holy Spirit in the Church: baptism, however, ought not to be repeated, the Apostle saying: One God, &c.

**f. ¶Baptized.** For then they had received nothing from [?]

**g. ¶One.** That is, incom[municable] [?]

**h. ¶One.** That is, from one [?]

**i. ¶One.** That is, one [?] whether for little children or for adults [?] hinders, or one, because in [?]

**k. ¶So that what.** Namely, the sa[crament]; the remission of sins [?] *infra ea. §. nemo*. For [?] [in time of] necessity he receives [it]. *32. dist. §. v[erum]*.

**¶But it must be noted. This is the 9th part of the question, in which it is said that some heretics do not baptize in the form of the Church. Other heretics give [baptism], but it is repeated.** Joan. de Fant., you say, [is] above the same, in particular at the chapter *non sanat*. They are understood to be baptized in the form of the Church, those who [?] have been baptized. And this he proves by the following chapter.

**l. ¶Of whom.** As the Arians, who say not [the same as] the Father. *24. q. 3. quidam. vers. Ariani.*

**m. ¶Of persons.** As the Sabellians, who [say] that the person of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Spirit is [one]. *3. quidam. versi. Sabelliani*.

**n. ¶Of the Church.** Which is necessary. *32. et inf. ea. c. de nube.*
