### What if it is the custom that a newly received canon must furnish a cope, or give a fixed sum of money for the church’s fabric, or for its lights, or for other things pertaining to the worship of God.

Hostiensis answers that such a praiseworthy custom ought to be observed. *Extra*, e[odem titulo], *Ad apostolicam*, and *Extra*, de preben., *Significatum*; the prebend, however, must not be retained as a pledge, argument from the same chapter. Or it may even properly be retained, if the custom is that the prebend is not assigned until he has furnished the cope. For in this case, once made a canon, the cleric can be required to furnish the spiritual \[cope\], so that it may be done — as a kind of permutation of spiritual for spiritual, as in *Extra*, de rerum permutatione, *Ad questiones*. And so in this exaction the private interest of the canons is reproved, but not the public interest, argument from *Extra*, de consti., *Cum omnes*, and c. *Ex parte*.

### What of a certain custom that has grown up in many churches — namely, that when canons are to be made, many, especially the senior ones, say to one another: “Let us make canons of our little nephews,” and perhaps add, “let it please you to choose one of mine, and I shall be pleased to choose one of yours” — and so they bargain by these or similar words.

Raymundus answers that simony is committed there, since there ought to be no pact and no agreement, 1 q. 2 *Quam pio*.

### What if one of them says: “While I live, no one will become a canon in this church unless the man for whom I have asked is made canon first, or along with the others.”

Raymundus answers that there is simony there, just as in the preceding case.

### What if one of them says: “It is the custom that, if you have been heard in one of your petitions, I ought to be heard in another.”

Raymundus answers that, if the requests are spiritual, there is no simony there, argument from di. 62 *Quis igitur*: “All who serve one Lord ought to enjoy equal liberty, since ‘a weight and a weight, a measure and a measure’ \[Proverbs 20:10\],” 22, *Extra*, de consti., *Cum omnes*; and argument to this effect from 16 q. 7 c. 1, and 25 q. 1 § *Is itaque*. *Respondet*. But if they are carnal, or arise from some pact made or to be made, there is simony there.
