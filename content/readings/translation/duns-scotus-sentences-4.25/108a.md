### Resolution of the foregoing contradiction of authorities.

Some resolve the matter in this way. They say that heretics, having received the priestly or episcopal unction, when they withdraw from the Church retain indeed the right of conferring Baptism, but do not have the power of conferring sacred Orders or of consecrating the Lord’s Body, after they have been cut off and condemned by the Church; just as a degraded Bishop has no power to bestow sacred Orders, although he has not lost the faculty of baptizing. What Augustinus says is understood to apply to those heretics who have been divided from the truth of the faith and from the unity of doctrine not by the sentence of the Church but by the perversity of their own opinion; though such, they nevertheless have the right of ordaining and consecrating. And those who are ordained by them before manifest excision, even if they have openly departed with them and have been condemned by the sentence of the Church, are nevertheless not to be ordained again if they return.
