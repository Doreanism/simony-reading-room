# Distinction 25

## (The Text of the Master of the Sentences.)

### On Those Ordained by Heretics.

It is also customary to ask whether heretics who have been cut off and condemned by the Church can confer sacred Orders, and whether those ordained by them, upon returning to the unity of the Church, ought to be reordained. The words of the Doctors make this question perplexing and almost insoluble, for they appear to disagree on many points. Some seem to maintain that heretics cannot confer sacred Orders, and that those who are seen to have been ordained by them do not receive grace. For Innocentius says: “The clergy of the Arians are not to be received with the dignity of the priesthood or of any ministry; only their Baptism, which is conferred in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, is permitted to be valid.” He also says that they “cannot give the Holy Spirit, whom they have lost”; and that “those ordained by heretics have a wounded head; and that he who has lost his honor cannot give honor, nor has the recipient received anything, since there was nothing in the giver that the other could have received.” He also holds that “to those coming from the heretics by the laying on of hands only lay communion is to be granted, and not one of these is to be admitted even to the smallest clerical honor.” Gregorius likewise
