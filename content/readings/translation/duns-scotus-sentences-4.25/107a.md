### He sets forth authorities that seem to oppose what has been said.

On the other hand, others seem to hold that even by heretics who have been cut off, sacred Orders, like Baptism, can be conferred; nor are those who have been ordained or baptized there, when they return from the heretics, to be ordained again, just as they are not to be baptized again. Hence Augustinus: “As to what some say concerning even condemned heretics, that ‘one who departs from the Church does not lose the Baptism he received, but loses the right of conferring what he received,’ this appears in many ways to be said in vain. First, because no reason is given why one who cannot lose that Baptism should be able to lose the right of conferring it. For each is a Sacrament, and each is given to a man by a certain consecration: the former when he is baptized, the latter when he is ordained. For this reason it is not permitted in the Catholic Church for either to be repeated.
