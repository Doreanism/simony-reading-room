For just as a rank properly so called denotes the effect of ordination, in ordering one toward the ministry of the sacraments proximately or remotely, and most especially of the Eucharist, so degradation properly so called is a movement toward the removal of the said rank as to its use, but not as to its bestowal absolutely, as the Doctor rightly \[says\] in the following section, concerning which something has been said in the preceding question.

Others distinguish between these \[terms\] in this way: that deposition, when it is the total removal from order, office, and the clerical state, stands as a sentence pronounced with a view to a real degradation, which is carried out by a certain solemnity that is the execution of the pronounced sentence, and by it the person is handed over to the secular judge to be punished according to the laws of the civil forum and the gravity of the crime. This distinction is also rightly proved from the chapter *Degradatio, de poenis* in the Sixth, which the Doctor cites, and from \[the chapter\] *Volumus, de verborum significatione* — although it could be said that degradation, or deposition itself, pertains to the solemnity and form of execution.

(d) *In what manner this penalty is inflicted*, etc. He responds that this penalty is never inflicted by the law itself — that is, as a removal from every state and ecclesiastical privilege — but \[that it is\] inflicted by a judge; which is the common opinion, and is evident from the form handed down in the chapter cited, *Degradatio, de poenis*, which requires the action of a judge and a sentence; such is the form: *By the authority of God almighty, of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and of our own, we take from you the clerical habit, and we strip you of every order, benefice, and clerical privilege*, etc. Moreover the ecclesiastical judge ought to intercede effectively for him, so that, short of the peril of death, the civil judge may moderate the sentence imposed on the accused, from chapter *Novimus, de verborum significatione*.
