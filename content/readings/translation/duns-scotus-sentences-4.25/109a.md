Some, however, hold that those heretics who were ordained in the Church have the right of ordaining and consecrating, even after they have been separated; but those who, being established in schism or heresy, have been ordained and anointed by them, lack that right, and therefore, when they wish to ordain, they inflict a wound rather than confer grace.

### On Simony: whence it is so called, and what it is?

Concerning simoniacs there can be no doubt that they are heretics, who nevertheless, before the sentence of degradation, both ordain and consecrate. And although those properly are called simoniacs who, after the manner of Simon Magus, wish to procure for a price the grace that cannot be priced, while those who, after the manner of Gehazi, receive a price for a sacred ministry ought to be called Gehazites; nevertheless all both the givers and the receivers are called simoniacs, and both are struck with the same sentence.

### On Those Ordained by Simoniacs, Whether Knowingly or Not.

There is a difference, however, between those who are ordained by simoniacs knowingly and those who are so ordained unknowingly. “For those who have knowingly allowed themselves to be consecrated by simoniacs — or rather to be execrated — their consecration is entirely null. But those who are ordained by simoniacs, whom at the time of their ordination they do not know to be simoniacs, and who are then reckoned as Catholics, their ordination is mercifully sustained.”
