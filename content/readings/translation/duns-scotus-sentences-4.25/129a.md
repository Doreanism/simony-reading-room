Scotus, however, makes little of the text cited for this purpose.

The fourth (u) penalty is excommunication. What it is has been said in distinction 19, in the fifth article of the solution. And this concerns major excommunication, which is simply excommunication; for minor excommunication is not excommunication except in a qualified sense, because in a qualified sense it excludes from the communion of the faithful — namely, in the Sacraments.

Whence also it is incurred, is clear: because sometimes it is by Law, sometimes by a judge. And as for that which is by Law, it was once possible to state the number — indeed it was a small number — when the Decretals were compiled under Gregory IX; but today who could count them, and as they grow to infinity with the increasing perversity of human heads, there is no need to fill up parchments on this matter.
