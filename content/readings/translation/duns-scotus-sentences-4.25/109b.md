### On Those Who Say They Are Buying Bodily, Not Spiritual, Things.

“If some should object that they are not buying the consecrations but the very things which proceed from the consecration, they are shown to be utterly foolish. For whoever sells one of these, without which the other is not had, leaves neither unsold.”

### On the Distinction of Simoniacs.

The simoniac heresy admits of a threefold distinction. For some are ordained simoniacally by simoniacs; others are ordained simoniacally by non-simoniacs; others are ordained non-simoniacally by simoniacs. Hence Pope Nicolaus: “We have established a decree concerning the threefold simoniac heresy, namely, concerning simoniacs who ordain simoniacally or are simoniacally ordained, and concerning simoniacs ordained simoniacally by non-simoniacs, and concerning those non-simoniacally ordained by simoniacs. Simoniacs simoniacally ordained or ordaining are, according to the Canons, to fall from their own grade; simoniacs also simoniacally ordained by non-simoniacs are likewise to be removed from office; but those simoniacs non-simoniacally ordained by simoniacs we mercifully permit, by the laying on of hands, to remain in office, for the necessity of the time. This is to be understood of those who are ordained by simoniacs without knowing them to be simoniacs. These persons it is not the guilt of a crime that makes simoniacs, but the ordination by a simoniac.”
