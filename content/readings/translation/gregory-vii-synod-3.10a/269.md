## Excommunication of the Bishops of Lombardy

“The bishops of Lombardy, who in contempt of canonical and apostolic authority conspired by oath against blessed Peter, prince of the apostles, we suspend by the authority of the same blessed Peter from every episcopal office and separate from the communion of the holy Church.”

## Excommunication of the Transalpine Bishops

“Berengar, bishop of Agde, because he communicated with the excommunicated bishop of Narbonne and performed episcopal functions in his place, we excommunicate. Hermann, bishop of Vienne, justly deposed for simony, perjuries, sacrileges, and apostasy, because he does not cease to harass the church of Vienne, we excommunicate. And upon the churches of Romans and Saint Irenaeus of Lyon, for as long as he shall occupy them, we lay an interdict on the divine office. Desiderius and the clerics of the church of Romans, who expelled our canons regular from it and communicated with excommunicates, we excommunicate from it until they make satisfaction. The abbot of Saint-Gilles likewise. And the count of Saint-Gilles on account of his blood-relation \[i.e., consanguineous marriage\]. And the count of Forez. And Humbert of Beaujeu, on account of his harassment of the church of Lyon. Stephen of Le Puy, simoniac and homicide, already excommunicated by our legates, and Pons of Grenoble, until he comes to his senses, we excommunicate. And those things which the bishop of Die, in the diocese of Die, has done concerning tithes and first-fruits and churches, and the other things which he established on our legation, we also confirm.”
