## Likewise the Excommunication of Henry, King of the Germans

Blessed Peter, prince of the apostles, incline, we beseech, your kindly ears to us and hear me your servant, whom you have nourished from infancy and, to this day, have delivered from the hand of the wicked, who have hated and still hate me for my fidelity to you. You are my witness, and my lady the mother of God, and your brother blessed Paul among all the saints, that your holy Roman Church drew me, unwilling, to its governance, and that I did not consider it robbery to ascend to your throne; rather, I would have preferred to end my life in pilgrimage than to seize your place by worldly cunning for the glory of the world. And therefore I believe that it is by your grace, not by my own works, that it has pleased you and still pleases you that the Christian people specially committed to you should obey me, as one specially entrusted with your office. And to me by your grace is given the power, bestowed by God, of binding and loosing in heaven and on earth. Trusting therefore in this confidence, for the honor and defense of your Church, on behalf of almighty God the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, through your power and authority I deny Henry the king, son of Henry the emperor, who has risen up against your Church with unheard-of pride, the governance of the whole kingdom of the Germans and of Italy; and I absolve all Christians from the bond of the oath they have sworn or shall swear to him; and I forbid anyone to serve him as king.
