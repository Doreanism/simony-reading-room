§. Likewise it is certain that none of the Apostles, except Paul, was the general Preacher of the whole world of the Gentiles; rather, they were each assigned individually to individual Provinces. Hence on Romans 1, on the words *Separated unto the Gospel of God*, the Gloss says: *Paul the Apostle was appointed Preacher to the Gentiles of the whole world; while the others were made Legates and Preachers to individual Provinces.* Likewise blessed Gregorius, though he was supreme Pontiff, nevertheless forbids himself to be called universal Pontiff; *because that is taken away from others which is bestowed on one beyond what reason requires*, as is read in dist. 99 can. *Ecce in præfatione*. It is not likely, therefore, that the Lord Pope, Vicar and imitator of Christ, has granted to an unlimited and undefined body of persons, neither canonically chosen by himself or by another Prelate, nor proved by experience, the general Apostolate or Pontificate of the whole world, or even a general Opitulatio or Legation; that is, by granting them in general the aforesaid free power; lest by this they should appear greater or more discerning than the Apostles, or than all the Bishops; and lest that should appear to be taken away from all the Prelates of the Church which would be bestowed on those unlimited and undefined persons beyond what reason requires; and so the name of Bishop would be cheapened, and the state of the Church disturbed. For, as Pope Damasus says, *if the name of the Pontiffs is cheapened, the whole state of the Church is thrown into confusion*.

§. Likewise in the Gospel of Matthew chapter 10, both the definite number and the definite names of the Apostles chosen by the Lord are expressed for this reason: that, apart from these, those who were to be False ones — as undefined and not chosen by the Lord — might be excluded. Hence on Matthew 10, on the words *The names of the twelve Apostles are these*, the Gloss says: *The catalogue of the Apostles is set down here so that, apart from these, those who were to be False ones might be excluded.* For the Apostles foresaw that many Pseudo-Apostles, and many false Teachers, would arise in the Church of God, and Preachers under the name of Christ
