§. Likewise, as Augustinus shows in the book *De Opere Monachorum*, from ch. 3 through ch. 12, it is certain that the power of taking sustenance from the Gospel is consequent upon the power of preaching; whence it cannot be taken away from the preacher, because the power of preaching and the power of living from the Gospel cannot be separated from one another. And this from the Lord’s precept in Deuteronomy 25, *Thou shalt not muzzle the mouth of the ox that treadeth out the corn.* Gloss: *That is, you shall not forbid the preacher to take what is necessary.* And in 1 Corinthians 9, upon the same word, *Thou shalt not muzzle the mouth of the ox that treadeth out the corn,* the Gloss says, *That is, you shall not forbid the preacher to live by his preaching.* For as is said in 1 Corinthians 9, *The Lord ordained that they who preach the Gospel should live by the Gospel.* Gloss: *That they may be the more unencumbered to preach the word of God.* And therefore Augustinus says in the book *De Opere Monachorum*, chs. 10, 11, 12, that *to preachers who have been sent, sustenance is owed from the Gospel by the Lord’s right, that is, by divine right.*
