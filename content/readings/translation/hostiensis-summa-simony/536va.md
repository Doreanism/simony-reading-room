I shall speak more fully. j. e. §. qua pena.

## But if prayers are offered, and on account of the prelacy the prayers are unjust

But if prayers are offered, and on account of the prelacy the prayers are unjust — especially when it is a layman whose countenance can be feared — as s. de ele. ostis. and c. quisqs, and de iure pat. ppterea. xvj. q. vij. si qs deinceps. Unless perhaps the prelate, with no regard to the prayers, installs him on his own office alone, the necessity or utility of the church demanding it, as s. de eta. et qua. tua.

## But where they are offered for a simple benefice, and he for whom they are offered is unworthy

But where they are offered for a simple benefice, and he for whom they are offered is unworthy, he commits simony. f. q. i. de ordinationibus and c. sunt nonulli.

## But if he is worthy and the prayers do not follow as the principal cause

But if he is worthy and the prayers do not follow as the principal cause, it is not simony. j. q. i. quibusda. ar. ad hoc, s. de eta. et qua. tuaz.

## Just as a spiritual right cannot be sold, so neither can it be redeemed

Just as a spiritual right cannot be sold, so neither can it be redeemed, as j. c. querela. Nor resigned for money, j. q. i. nullus eps. ij. For in all these every transaction and every pact is reproved, as s. dixi et probat. ff. de pac. cum pride. and de transactionib super eo. xvj. q. vij.

## But may I redeem a wrong done to me without fear of this crime

But may I redeem a wrong done to me without fear of this crime — suppose a bishop unjustly excommunicates me or threatens to take away a benefice which I unjustly possess; I give him money so that he may revoke the unjust sentence or desist from this wrong, and indeed I lawfully redeem my right although he unlawfully receives \[the money\]. ar. j. q. iij. quesitum. j. de magistris phibeas, and de vsur. c. f. “Let us redeem the time, according to the Apostle, for the days are evil.” ar. j. c. ad aures and c. dilectus, and j. and C. mandati p diuersas, and ff. de bonis coruz q ante Iniam mor. sibi cosci. l. f.
