## But if by another’s prayers

one is promoted, and \[those prayers are\] spiritual, it does not induce simony. *ar. xliij. dist. principali. j.q.j. latore.* Unless consanguinity draws them in. *s.q.s. de ordinationibus.* *argu. s. de preben. graue*, and *vt bene.ecc.vt nostra.* For unjust love of the heart induces simony. *j.q.j. si quis neque sanctis*, and *c. sunt nonnulli*, where it is said \[that\] they take the place of a price. T. et H. *naturali* — for since nothing is given etc., he does not lose the simony charge, *j.q.j. impio.* Which I understand when the kinsman is unworthy; otherwise the prelate is the more strongly bound to provide for him than for a stranger. *argu. s. de coha. cleri. et mu. c.j.* “Therefore do not despise your blood, that is, one worthy and lawful.” *j.q.j. filium* and *c. quibusdam, ad fi.* But the prelates of our time, embracing this too much, today reckon almost nothing worthy except their own blood. Hence Bernardus: “A marvelous thing — bishops have someone to whom they entrust souls; but to whom they may entrust temporal things they do not find. For a bishop will readily entrust many thousands of souls to some little nephew of his, to whom he would not entrust two pears.”

## But if someone for me

offers or promises prayers or money — when I have expressly forbidden it — it does not harm me. as *j.e. sicut nobis. §. pe.*, and *de homicidio. pe. trua.* Otherwise if it is done with my knowledge and acquiescence: for then I am said to interpose my disturbed will, and therefore however secretly it may be done I ought to renounce, if I wish to save my soul — mindful of that word, “It profits a man nothing if he gain the whole world and suffer the loss of his own soul.” *vt.j.c. mattheus.*

## But if this happens entirely without my knowledge

and by an enemy wishing to impede my promotion, which he hopes \[to prevent\], I do not lose \[the benefice\] by simony, as regards myself, lest he should reap from his own deceit what he intends. as *j.co. sicut nobis. §. pe.* and *c. nobis fuit. rh.j.*

## But if my friend has given money

I am simoniacally promoted but I am not a simoniac; yet I am bound to renounce. as *j.c. de simoniace.* and *c. o regularib\[us\]*
