## And Whence It Is Named

†a And indeed it is called simony from Simon Magus, who first in the New Testament wished to buy grace from blessed Peter. It is also called the *cupiditas Gieziti*, that is, the cupidity of Gehazi, from Gehazi the servant of Elisha, who first in the Old Testament sold the grace of healing to Naaman, the prince of the army of the king of the Assyrians, whence he was struck with the leprosy from which Naaman had suffered and from which Naaman was healed, as at C. 1 q. 1 *Quod studet*, and cap. *Cito turpem*. And therefore it is said that such men receive their portion with Gehazi and Simon, at \[Extra\] eodem \[titulo\] *Non satis*, cap. seq., and cap. *Sicut pro certo*. So too one who takes honour upon himself is bidden to share with Dathan and Abiron, whom the earth swallowed alive, as at \[Extra\] *de electione*, *Sed evitanda* §*Si quis autem*. ¶ From what has been said it is clear that, properly speaking, the seller of spiritual things is a “Gehazite” and the buyer truly a “simoniac.” But usage has prevailed that both are called simoniacs, according to Goffredus.

## How It Is Committed
