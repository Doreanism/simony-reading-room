Hence Paul, 1 Corinthians 9: *Qui altari deserviunt cum altari participant, ita et Dominus ordinavit his qui evangelium annunciant de evangelio vivere.* Those who serve at the altar shall be partakers of the altar, in such a way that God has ordained that those who preach the gospel should live by the gospel—not that they should sell it, for that would be simony, as has been said.

Sixth, mark well that no man should presume to perform the office of priests and of those who are in holy orders; for if he does so wrongly, the same will befall him as befell King Uzziah. For after he had coveted and presumed to perform the office, and had taken the censer and burned incense and threatened the priests because they opposed him, leprosy clung to him there in the precinct within the house of God, etc., as it is written, 2 Chronicles 26. See 85.g.

Seventh, a man should not sell the gifts of grace, nor the virtues that are in the soul, nor knowledge, for that would be simony. Augustinus says: *Spectat ad officium vestrae dignitatis gratis pete tibi dona dare gratis: sed si uni fidei munera vendatis in cursores Giezi lepra vos sciatis.* It belongs to the office of your dignity to give gifts freely to those who ask for grace; but if you in any way sell the gifts of faith, know that you fall under the leprosy of Gehazi. So says Saint Augustinus to the ministers of the church. Gehazi was the disciple of Elisha, who took a great gift from the rich man Naaman because his master had healed him of his leprosy.
