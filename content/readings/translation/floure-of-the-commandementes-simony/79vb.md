G. Again, we read in 2 Maccabees 4 how King Antiochus sold to Jason the dignity of being high priest (that is, bishop), and harm came to him for it. The prophet Isaiah says in his fifth chapter: *Ue qui iustificatis impium, pro muneribus et iustitiam iusti aufertis ab eo*—”Cursed are you who justify the wicked for gifts and take away the justice of the just.” Those who enter into benefices wrongfully by the force and power of princes or great lords, or by threats, complaints, or by their entreaties and petitions, and not by the door, which is Jesus Christ, are thieves. For through fear of the power or favor of these lords, just right is corrupted, the good are driven out, and the unworthy are beneficed—and harm comes to them. We have an example in 1 Maccabees 7, of how the accursed Alchimus was made high priest by King Demetrius. He did many evils. And the said Alchimus died in a cursed state, as it is written in 1 Maccabees 9. This example is recorded later on. *Quere lxxxv.h.*

C. Again, some enter into a benefice wrongfully through flatteries, false interpretations, and deceptions. And inasmuch as they do not enter by the door, which is Jesus Christ, they are thieves.
