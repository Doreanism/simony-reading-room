But, as a figure of all those who sell or buy spiritual things, both he and his money were cursed by the mouth of Saint Peter; and because he was the first in the New Testament who wished to buy a spiritual thing, this species or kind of theft is called simony throughout the whole holy Church, on account of the accursed Simon, who is the figure of all the damned simoniacs. By selling or buying a spiritual thing one should understand every bargain or contract that is not made simply and purely for the love of God, but is made chiefly for gifts, prayers, services, or for any other temporal thing. Likewise, by selling or buying spiritual things one is to understand here the grace of the Holy Spirit and the virtues that are in the soul, the spirit of prophecy, the office of preaching, the celebration of the divine office in the church, and the administration of the holy sacraments. By things annexed to the spiritual one is to understand burial places, tithes, chalices, corporals, towels, albs, and other vessels or hallowed vestments, which may be sold or exchanged to other churches, though not at a higher price because they are hallowed; otherwise it would be simony. Likewise cures, prebends, canonries, chapels, the right of patronage considered simply in itself, the right to receive tithes and oblations on account of a benefice of the church, and the authority or office to serve a cure or other prebend of the church: all these things have an annexation to holy orders, and they may not be sold in the manner described above without there being simony, which is a capital crime according to the law and is likened to heresy, *ut habet i. ca. q. i.
