# Of Simony, Which God Forbids

The masters define simony in this way: *Simonia est studiosa voluntas emendi vel vendendi spirituale vel annexum spirituali.* A person commits simony by selling or buying a spiritual thing, or a thing annexed to the spiritual, through a bargain of gift, promise, service, prayers, undue petitions, or any other temporal thing. Both God and nature deny that a spiritual thing may be exchanged for, or bought with, a corporeal and temporal thing, whatever its price and worth may be, as will be explained below.
