But these things, briefly spoken here, are enough for people of good conscience to make them inquire more fully into the occasions and various ways in which this matter may befall or happen to them. Moreover, simoniacal people should dread malediction and excommunication. They should also dread irregularity, which will not be expounded here for the sake of brevity. They should also dread to be found guilty in the judgment before God. They should also dread to lose the joys of paradise. They should also dread punishment and damnation. There is an example of a man who was damned and who held many benefices: *Quere lxxxv.e.* Another example, of how few curates are saved: *Quere lvi.g.* They should also dread to be sent to abide in the fire of hell forever. As for those who have offended through simony, if they turn to penance they shall have grace and mercy. They should also give alms, for that is a good remedy.
