Likewise Hebrews 13: *Do not forget beneficence and fellowship, for by such sacrifices God is won over;* therefore alms must be given to any poor mendicant.

Likewise 1 John 3: *He who has the substance of this world, and shall see his brother in need, and shall shut up his bowels from him, how does the charity of God abide in him?* Therefore alms must be given to any poor mendicant.

Likewise Matthew 5: *Give to him who asks of you;* and Luke 6: *Give to everyone who asks of you;* therefore alms must be given to any poor mendicant.

Likewise 2 Corinthians 8: *Let your abundance,* namely bodily, *supply their want, that their abundance,* namely of their merits, who devote themselves to divine things, *may be the supplement of your want,* namely spiritual, *that there may be equality.* Gloss: For those who live in this age do not have merits sufficient for eternal life, unless they are aided by the poor of Christ, whose is the kingdom of heaven, whom they have made friends and debtors to themselves; therefore alms must not be denied to anyone.

Likewise 1 Timothy 6: *Charge the rich of this world to give easily;* therefore alms must not be denied to anyone.

Likewise Acts 2: *They sold their possessions and goods, and divided them to all, according as everyone had need;* therefore alms must be given according to the necessity of anyone.

Likewise Romans 15: *Now therefore I shall go to Jerusalem to minister to the Saints.* Gloss: These had given themselves wholly to divine services, caring nothing for worldly things, and were giving an example of good conduct to the faithful. And below: *For if the Gentiles have been made partakers of their spiritual things, they ought to minister to them in carnal things;* Gloss: That is, to the Jews who sent them as Preachers. If they ought to, therefore they cannot refuse.

Likewise, that alms must especially be given to a poor preacher, is plain from Galatians 6: *Let him who is catechized in the word communicate to him who catechizes him, in all good things.* Gloss: Let him make all good things common with him, for it is an established precept that the one to whom preaching is given should furnish necessities to the Preacher of the word of God.

Likewise in the same place: *But especially to those of the household of the faith.* Gloss: Mercy must be shown to all, in which the just are to be preferred, who are of the faith.
