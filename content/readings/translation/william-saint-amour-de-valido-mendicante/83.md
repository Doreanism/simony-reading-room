*wise weakness of body is more readily to be aided; likewise if anyone has fallen from wealth into poverty; and especially if not by his own fault, but has lost what he had either by robberies, or proscription, or false accusations.”* Therefore only to such persons should it be given.

Likewise, Augustinus, *Ad Vincentium Donatistam*: *”More usefully is bread taken away from the hungry man, if, being secure about his food, he neglects justice, than that bread is broken for him, in order that, seduced, he may acquiesce in injustice.”* But he who is unwilling to work bodily to seek a livelihood, when he can work; or he who can have a livelihood from elsewhere without sin; if he begs for a livelihood, acts unjustly, because contrary to the doctrine of the Apostle, as I shall prove. Therefore from such a one bread ought to be withdrawn; therefore not given.

The minor premise is proved. 2 Thessalonians 3: *”If any will not work, neither let him eat.”* Gloss: *”The Apostle wishes the servants of God to work bodily, whereby they may live, lest they be compelled by want to beg for necessities.”* Therefore if such a one begs, he acts unjustly, contrary to the Apostle.

Likewise, the Apostle commands that such persons be avoided in the same place: *”If any man obey not our word”* — namely concerning acquiring a livelihood by laboring bodily, if he has nothing else from which he may live — *”note that man by an epistle”* — Gloss: *”that I may be able to rebuke him”* — *”AND DO NOT keep company with him, that he may be ashamed”* — Gloss: *”that he may blush, being cast off by all, and so subject himself to the precepts of the Apostle, namely concerning working bodily for a livelihood.”* Therefore such a one acts unjustly.

Likewise, 1 Thessalonians 4: *”Work with your own hands, that you want nothing of any man’s.”* Gloss: *”One must work, not be idle, so that you do not desire another’s property, much less beg for it.”* Therefore he who thus begs acts contrary to the Apostle; therefore he acts unjustly.

Likewise, 1 Thessalonians 4: *”We beseech you, brethren, that you give heed to be quiet”* — Gloss: *”from curiosity”* — *”AND THAT you do your own business”* — Gloss: *”setting aside the affairs of others; which is useful to you for the amendment of life”* — *”AND WORK with your own hands”* — Gloss: *”which helps you to be quiet; for that evil comes from idleness.”* Therefore he who does not do these things begs contrary to the doctrine of the Apostle; therefore he acts unjustly; therefore bread is to be withdrawn from him; therefore not to be given.

Likewise, Luke 12: *”Sell what you possess, and give alms.”* Gloss: *”Sell your possessions, so that, all your goods having once been despised for the Lord, afterwards you may work by the labor of your hands, whereby you may live, and may give alms.”* Therefore after the sale of all things one must not beg, but work; therefore he who does not do this acts contrary to the doctrine of the Church; therefore he acts unjustly.
