# On the Able-Bodied Mendicant. A Question.

It is asked whether we ought to give alms to an able-bodied mendicant, if he is poor.

*That it is so:* Job 31: *If I have denied the poor what they desired;* therefore alms must be given to any poor mendicant.

Likewise Proverbs 21: *He who stops his ear against the cry of the poor shall also himself cry, and shall not be heard;* therefore alms must be given to any poor mendicant.

Likewise Ecclesiasticus 29: *On account of the commandment, take up the poor man, and on account of his neediness do not send him away empty;* therefore alms must be given to any poor mendicant.

Likewise Matthew 25: *What you have done to one of the least of mine, you have done to me;* therefore alms must not be denied to anyone for Christ’s sake.

Likewise Romans 12: *Communicating with the necessities of the saints;* therefore it is good to bestow alms generously, and it is a work of charity.
