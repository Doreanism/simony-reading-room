Likewise, Augustinus, *To Vincentius the Donatist*, *23 q. 4 c. Nimium*: *Often the wicked have persecuted the good, and the good the wicked; the former by harming through injustice, the latter by chastising through discipline*. And a little later: *The wicked killed the prophets, and the prophets killed the wicked; the Jews scourged Christ, and Christ scourged the Jews*. And a little later: *In all these things, what is to be considered, except which of them acted for Truth? which for iniquity? which for the sake of harming? which for the sake of correcting?* Therefore from those who act contrary to the doctrine of the Apostle, bread should be withdrawn, so that they may be corrected; for whoever does this pursues the wicked.

Likewise, prove that preachers cannot ask for anything. 1 Thessalonians 2: *Nor were we ever an occasion of avarice*. Gloss: *I do not say in avarice; but I neither did nor said anything in which there might be an occasion of avarice*. But in asking for money or for provisions, there is an occasion of avarice; therefore to ask is contrary to the doctrine of the Apostle; therefore nothing ought to be given to those who ask.

Likewise, a preacher ought not to be \[engaged\] in flattering speech. 1 Thessalonians 2: *Nor were we ever in flattering speech*. Gloss: *On account of which others please men*. But he who continually, or frequently, asks for his food, must of necessity flatter: 2 Thessalonians 3: *That we might give ourselves to you as a pattern for imitating us*. Gloss: *He who frequently comes to another’s table, given over to idleness, must of necessity flatter the one who feeds him; whereas the Christian religion summons men to liberty*. Therefore he who is a preacher ought not frequently to ask for his food; therefore if he asks, nothing ought to be given.

Likewise, 2 Corinthians 12: *I seek not yours, but you*. Gloss: *Not riches in gold and silver, but to save you*. Therefore preachers ought to ask for nothing.

Likewise, Philippians, last \[chapter\]: *I do not seek the gift, but the fruit*. Therefore even true apostles ought to ask for nothing.

Likewise: He who receives food in return for any spiritual thing, whether before or after, commits simony. *1 q. 3 c. Si quis praebendas*. Therefore for preaching, even after the preaching, it is not lawful for him to receive food to whom it is not owed — that is, for him who is not an apostle. For *accipere* is to seize at any time whatever, *1 q. 1 c. Eos, qui per pecunias*. Therefore if he asks, nothing ought to be given.
