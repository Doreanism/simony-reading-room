# Gregory VII to the People of Lodi

We rejoice, most beloved, in your devout religious zeal, for we have heard that, kindled by zeal for the divine law, you have risen up in praiseworthy fashion against the detestable simoniacal heresy and the fornication of priests. We rejoice likewise concerning our brother and fellow bishop, your pastor lord Opizo, who has shown himself to us to burn so greatly with that same zeal of piety that he has earnestly begged the assistance of this holy see in order to accomplish these things. We therefore exhort you, and admonish you as our dearest sons, that, in crushing and utterly rooting out these abominations—namely, the simoniacal heresy and the fornication of the ministers of the sacred altar—you stand fast as devout sons together with your aforesaid pastor, our brother, for the honor of almighty God and of holy Church and for the salvation of your whole land; that you also lend him your support with all your strength in ordering the churches rightly and canonically; and indeed that in all matters which he resolves to undertake for the honor of God and your salvation, you obediently submit to him, so that the purity of your holy mother the Church may flourish among you—the Church which Christ joined to himself as a chaste bride, without spot or wrinkle. Whoever shall keep these things, let him with confidence presume upon the grace of almighty God and know that he has the apostolic blessing. But whoever shall attempt to oppose him in the matters we have set forth, and shall fail to assist him in all things that are of God, let him tremble lest he incur the vengeance of divine censure and apostolic malediction in place of the grace and blessing which he does not care to possess.
