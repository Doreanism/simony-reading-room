Gloss: *He who frequently gathers at another’s table, given to idleness, must of necessity flatter the one who feeds him; whereas our religion, namely the Christian religion, calls men to liberty.* Those, therefore, who willingly and frequently gather at another’s table, together with bodily idleness, do not appear to be true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.

The thirty-fourth sign is that True Apostles do not hate men, even their enemies and those who hate them, according to the teaching of the Lord, Matthew 5: *Love your enemies, and do good to those who hate you*. But pseudo-prophets injure their neighbors and defame them. Whence on Jeremiah 23, *From the prophets of Jerusalem pollution has gone forth over all the earth*, the Gloss says: *It is not enough for them to injure their neighbors, but those whom they once hate, they strive to defame throughout the whole world*. Those preachers, therefore, who hate those whom they consider enemies, and defame them, are not true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.

The thirty-fifth sign is that, when the pseudo-apostles are examined or tested as to whether they are True Apostles or liars, they do not patiently endure what the True Apostles do; but rather they persecute those who wish to test them in this way, and join themselves together through earthly powers to persecute them. Just as certain pseudo-apostles did in the primitive Church against the bishop of Ephesus, to whom the Lord says, Apocalypse 2: *I know*, that is, I APPROVE, *thy works, and thy labor*, that is, thy tribulation; *and how thou canst not bear them that are evil*; Gloss: *without correcting them or expelling them*; *and thou hast tried them, who say they are apostles and are not*; Gloss: *That is, those who say they are sent by God, in order more easily to deceive*. *And hast found them liars, and hast patience*; Gloss: *In the evils which they themselves, joined together through earthly powers, bring upon you*.

The thirty-sixth sign is that True Apostles do not go to preach to those who have already been converted by others; but rather they gird themselves to convert those who have not yet been converted by others, lest they build upon another’s foundation. So Blessed Paul did, Romans 15: *Thus have I labored, that from Jerusalem round about, as far as the Illyrian sea, I have replenished the Gospel*. Gloss: *that is, I have preached it fully*.
