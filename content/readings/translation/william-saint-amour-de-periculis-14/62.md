The fourteenth sign is that the true apostles do not flatter men for the sake of gain, as the pseudo-apostles flatter. Of these it is said in Ezekiel 13: *Woe to them that sew cushions under every elbow, and make pillows for the head of every age.* Gloss: *that is, sweet flattery;* AND THEY VIOLATED ME TOWARD MY PEOPLE FOR A HANDFUL OF BARLEY AND A PIECE OF BREAD. Gloss: *that is, for the refreshment of the belly;* THAT THEY MIGHT KILL THE SOULS THAT DO NOT DIE. Gloss: *You would call them dead if nothing were placed in their mouth;* AND MAKE ALIVE THE SOULS THAT DO NOT LIVE. Gloss: *The false prophets prophesied death and misfortune to the just who live in the Lord and gave them nothing, and prophesied life and prosperity to the unjust who are dead through sin, on account of their gifts.* Likewise 2 Corinthians 6: *Let us exhibit ourselves as ministers of God.* Gloss: *The ministers of God do not flatter, as the pseudo-apostles do who are eager for gain.* And therefore the true apostles do not frequent the courts of princes and magnates, knowing that text of Matthew 11: *Behold, they that are clothed in soft garments are in the houses of kings.* Gloss: *A strict life and preaching ought to avoid the palaces of the soft, which those clothed in soft garments frequent, flattering.* Those preachers, therefore, who dwell in courts, or flatter elsewhere, are not true apostles but pseudo-apostles.

The fifteenth sign is that the true apostles do not circumvent men so that they may give them temporal goods, whether in life or in death; indeed, even when asked they are unwilling to receive. Hence in 2 Corinthians 8 the Apostle says, *With much entreaty begging of us.* Gloss: *When, fearing for them, I would not consent that we should allow them to have the grace and fellowship of the ministry that is done unto the saints, they begged us that they might be permitted to give their own goods, not as their own, but as common.* The pseudo-apostles, on the contrary, either ask importunately or at least take away by smooth speech. 2 Corinthians 7: *We have circumvented no man.* Gloss: *By a fraudulent removal of their property, as the pseudo-apostles do who carry off your goods by smooth talk.* Likewise Matthew 24\[?\]: *Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, who devour the houses of widows, praying long prayers.* Gloss: *You who by your superstition aim at nothing except to make a prey of the people subject to you.* And Luke 20: *Who devour the houses of widows,*
