Since, then, the truth of divine Scripture is infallible — Matthew 24: *Heaven and earth shall pass away; but my words shall not pass away* — and the Holy Spirit, who spoke in the Apostle prophesying (as was shown above in chapter 1), cannot lie — *For prophecy came not by the will of man at any time; but the holy men of God spoke, inspired by the Holy Spirit,* as we read in 2 Peter 1 — it remains that all who are bound to defend the Church should rise to her defense, according to that passage in Proverbs 24: *Deliver those who are led to death; and cease not to free those who are dragged to destruction.* Nor let them put forward frivolous excuses, for it is said in the same place: *If you shall say, my strength does not suffice, he who is the inspector of the heart himself understands, and nothing escapes the savior of your soul, and he shall render to every one according to his works.* For whatever perishes in the Church through the failure of the Pastors will all be required of them at the Judgment, as Jacob acknowledges to Laban, whose sheep he was pasturing — Genesis 31: *I made good every loss to you, and whatever was lost by theft, you required of me.* And Ezekiel 3: *His blood will I require from your hand* — this is said to the Pastor, or Prelate.

If, however, the other things mentioned do not move the carnal Prelates, this at least should move them: that since the spiritual power of the Church — which consists in carrying out preaching, in hearing confessions, in imposing penances — is gradually being snatched from them (*for the wolf eats the lamb piece by piece*, 13. q. 1.), once it has been wholly transferred from those Prelates themselves to those who, by reason of their Order, or Profession, or Apostolic concession, already arrogate that power to themselves, then surely the jurisdiction over forensic matters — which is lucrative to those Prelates — and indeed the possession of the temporal goods of the Church, will not remain with those Prelates much longer. For why should they have the carnal goods of the Church, who do not minister the spiritual? Especially since it is said in 1 Corinthians 9: *Do you not know, that they who work in the holy place eat the things that are of the holy place; and they who serve the altar partake with the altar?* Therefore, just as the body does not live without the soul, so neither will the corporeal goods of the Church long stand without the spiritual. 1. q. 1. cap.
