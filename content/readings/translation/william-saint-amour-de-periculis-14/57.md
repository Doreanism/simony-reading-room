# Many signs, some infallible and some probable, by which the aforesaid men may be recognized. CHAPTER XIV.

Concerning the fourteenth point, beyond what has already been set forth, since the aforesaid Seducers will claim to be Apostles, or sent by God to preach and to save souls through their ministry — according to the saying of the Apostle in 2 Corinthians 11: *Such pseudo-apostles are deceitful workers, transforming themselves into the Apostles of Christ* — we shall therefore set out certain signs, some infallible and some probable, by which pseudo-apostles may be distinguished from the true Apostles of Christ.

The first sign is that True Apostles do not creep into houses, nor lead captive little women laden with sins, as the pseudo-apostles do. 2 Timothy 3: *Of these sort are they who creep into houses, and lead captive little women laden with sins.* This has been expounded above, in chapters 2 and 5. Those Preachers, therefore, who creep into houses and lead captive little women laden with sins, are not true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.

The second sign is that True Apostles do not deceive the hearts of the simple with contrived words by which they commend their own traditions, as the pseudo-apostles do. Romans, last chapter: *By sweet speeches and blessings they seduce the hearts of the innocent.* Gloss: *With contrived words they commend their own traditions, by which they deceive the hearts of the simple.* Indeed, they so seduce the hearts of the simple that they make them enter their sect, which they call a Religion; and those who previously lived in simplicity become, after their entrance, cunning, hypocrites, pseudo-apostles, and creepers into houses along with them — and sometimes they become worse than they. Hence Matthew 23: *Woe to you, Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you compass sea and land to make one proselyte; and when he is made, you make him the child of hell twofold more than yourselves.* Those, therefore, who do this are not true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.
