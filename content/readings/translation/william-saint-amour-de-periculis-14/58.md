The third sign is that True Apostles, if they are reproved, bear it patiently. 2 Corinthians 12: *The signs of my apostleship have been wrought upon you in all patience.* Gloss: *He calls patience to mind, which pertains to the character of a Preacher.* Those, therefore, who do not endure correction are not true Apostles; indeed, they show themselves not to be Christians. 1 Corinthians 3 \[i.e., 1 Corinthians 12\]: *No one can say, THE LORD JESUS, except in the Holy Spirit.* Gloss: *Christians ought to be humbled, that they may suffer themselves to be reproved, and not be soothed by flatteries.* Likewise, such men show themselves to be carnal, although they pretend to be spiritual. Galatians 4: *Have I then become your enemy, because I tell you the truth?* Gloss: *No carnal man wishes to be reproved when he errs.* Those Preachers, therefore, who do not endure correction do not appear to be true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.

The fourth sign is that True Apostles do not commend themselves. 2 Corinthians 4: *We preach not ourselves;* and 2 Corinthians 10 \[printed as 19\]: *We dare not match or compare ourselves with certain who commend themselves.* Gloss: *as the pseudo-apostles, who commend themselves by certain arts, and not God commending them.* Likewise, true Apostles, even though by good works they render themselves commendable to every conscience of men — not merely to the eyes — as the Apostle says in 2 Corinthians 4: *Commending ourselves to every conscience of men before God;* nevertheless do not commend themselves in comparison with others. Hence the Gloss in the same place: *Making ourselves commendable without comparison with others.* Indeed, they prefer the virtues of others to their own. Hence 2 Peter, last chapter: *As also our most dear brother Paul, according to the wisdom given him by God, has written to you.* Gloss: *The first of the Apostles, forgetful of his own primacy and of the keys given to him, marvels at the wisdom given to Paul; for it is the custom of the elect that they should marvel at the virtues of others more than at their own.* And Philippians 2: *In humility, esteeming others better than themselves.* Those, therefore, who do the contrary, saying that their state or their traditions are better than others’, if they are Preachers, are not true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.

The fifth sign is that True Apostles do not need letters of commendation, nor do they cause themselves to be commended by men to other men through letters. 2 Corinthians 3: *Do we need, as some do, letters of commendation?* Gloss: *as the pseudo-apostles.* Therefore those who do the contrary are not true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.

The sixth sign is that True Apostles do not preach unless they have been sent. Romans 10: *How shall they preach, unless they be sent?* Gloss: *None*
