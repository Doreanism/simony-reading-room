Proverbs 7: *Keep my law as the apple of your eye; bind it on your fingers, and write it on the tablets of your heart.* And on that verse of Psalm 118, *The law of your mouth is good to me, above thousands of gold and silver*, the Gloss says: Christ loves the Law of God more than greed loves thousands of gold and silver. Whoever, therefore, seeing the Law of Christ trampled upon and abolished through that accursed gospel which they call the *Eternal Gospel*, neglects and despises \[this\], or perhaps by dissimulation consents to it; and yet, for the sake of preserving or seeking his temporal honor, does not shrink from undertaking lawsuits and incurring scandals — such a person does not appear to be an Apostle of Christ, but a pseudo-apostle, if he is a Preacher.

The twenty-eighth sign is that the True Apostles do not seek out the more sumptuous lodgings, or those where they would be better catered for; but rather the more honorable, and those where it is given to them more gladly. And they receive only from those whom they believe will consider it a greater favor to themselves that their gift be accepted than if a gift were given to them. According to Matthew 10: *Into whatever city or town you enter, ask who in it is worthy, and there remain until you depart.* Gloss: A host should be chosen by the testimony of the neighbors, lest preaching be harmed by his ill repute. Nor should one run from house to house, but go straight to him *who is worthy*. Gloss: One who knows that he receives more grace than he gives — that is, one who gives gladly for Christ’s sake, not for some other advantage. Likewise, the True Apostles do not receive from those who still lie in their sins, but from those who have amended their lives. Hence 2 Corinthians 8: *But they first gave themselves to the Lord.* Gloss: Those who, amending their former errors and vices of conduct, vowed themselves to God, and then offered their gifts to the Brethren; for otherwise nothing should have been accepted from them, because *gifts blind the eyes and pervert the judgments of authority*; and those who give in order not to be reproved have no reward for their giving. Therefore those Preachers who seek out lodgings where they will be better fed, and who receive the gifts of wicked rich men in order to cover up their evils, or who receive the gifts of those who give more in order to be rid of the nuisance, or out of present embarrassment, than for God’s sake — these are not true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles. According to 2 Corinthians 9: *God loves a cheerful giver.* Gloss: He who gives something out of present shame, in order to be free of the weariness of those who keep asking, loses both the thing and the merit; whence he who receives from such a one does not seek the fruit but the gift — contrary to that saying of the Apostle in Philippians 4: *I do not seek the gift, but I seek the fruit.*
