The twenty-ninth sign is that the True Apostles do not labor to manage other people’s affairs in order to be fed from them; for the god of such men is their belly, according to 2 Thessalonians 3: *We have heard that some among you walk disorderly, doing no work, but meddling busily.* Gloss: in other people’s affairs; and in this way they deserve to be fed. Such conduct is abhorrent to the Lord’s discipline; for the god of those who provide for their necessities by foul concern is their belly. Therefore those Preachers who do such things are not true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.

The thirtieth sign is that the True Apostles do not rejoice over miracles or other more remarkable deeds which the Lord works through them, so as to wish to be honored on account of them; but rather they rejoice in the salvation which they await from the Lord. According to Luke 10: *Rejoice not in this, that the spirits are subject to you; but rejoice that your names are written in heaven.* Therefore those who boast of their miracles, or those of their own \[order\], and of the fact that through their ministry many are saved, as they say — these do not appear to be true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.

The thirty-first sign is that the True Apostles never seek their own glory in this life, but the glory of Christ. According to John 7: *He who speaks of himself seeks his own glory; but he who seeks the glory of him who sent him* — that is, of him by whom he says he was sent — *is truthful.* Therefore those who seek the things that pertain to the glory of this world (one of which is to attend upon those who reign, according to that saying of Boethius in the *Consolation*: Those who desire to be exalted in power either themselves reign, or strive to cling to those who reign; another is to wish to win where there is no reward with God; hence on that verse of Galatians 5: *Let us not become desirous of vainglory*, the Gloss says: It is vain boasting to wish to win where there is no reward) — insofar as they seek such things, do not appear to be true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.

The thirty-second sign is that the True Apostles flee the solemnities of men, and salutations, and banquets. According to Hieronymus to Nepotianus: I beseech you, whether you are here or there, flee the throngs of men, salutations, courtesies, and banquets, as if they were certain chains of pleasure. Therefore those who seek and love the company and banquets and attentions of secular potentates and the wealthy do not appear to be true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.
