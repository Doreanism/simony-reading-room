And therefore it is greatly to be feared that the ship in which they are, that is, the Church, may be endangered, unless they themselves are cast out, or go on their proper mission. And therefore not without cause did the Apostle say of such pseudo-apostles, 2 Timothy 3: *And avoid these.* Gloss: *Such as these.* As if he were saying: while they are such, that is, pseudo-apostles, avoid them.

The thirty-eighth is that True Apostles do not speak boastfully, nor attribute anything to themselves except what God does through them. Romans 15: *For I dare not speak of any of those things which Christ has not wrought by me.* Gloss: *That is, I speak only those things which Christ accomplishes through me, that is, through my ministry.* Those, therefore, who speak many things boastfully and attribute to themselves many things which are not done through them, are not true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.

The thirty-ninth is that True Apostles neither aim at nor rely upon logical or philosophical arguments. 1 Corinthians 2: *I came not in loftiness of speech or of wisdom.* Gloss: *I did not come in order to use logical or philosophical arguments.* Those preachers, therefore, who aim at and rely upon such arguments, are not true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.

The fortieth is that True Apostles do not love carnal things, but hate whatever stands in their way in the service of God. Luke 14: *He who does not hate his father, and mother, and brother, and sisters; and moreover his own soul also for my sake, cannot be my disciple.* That is: he who does not hate whatever stands in his way in the service of God is not worthy of being my disciple, nor can he remain in that discipleship. Since, then, true Apostles are true disciples of the Lord, it follows that those preachers who promote their nephews and kinsmen — however unworthy — to ecclesiastical benefices and honors against God’s will, or who do anything else that stands in their way in the service of God, are not true Apostles, but pseudo-apostles.
