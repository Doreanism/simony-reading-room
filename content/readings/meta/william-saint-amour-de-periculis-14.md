Chapter 14 of William of Saint-Amour's *De Periculis Novissimorum Temporum* (1256), enumerating the many signs—some infallible, others probable—by which pseudo-apostles may be distinguished from true apostles of Christ. Sign 20 holds that true apostles do not take the temporal goods of those to whom they preach, on pain of committing simony in the manner of Gehazi.
