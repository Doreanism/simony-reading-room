The *De simonia* titulus (Book VI, Titulus 55) of Astesanus of Asti's *Summa de casibus conscientiae* — the last titulus of Book VI, which treats the *sacramentum ordinis* and ecclesiastical persons and things. After surveying the excesses of prelates against their subjects and vice versa in the preceding titulus, Astesanus turns to simony, which he treats *specialiter* in seven parts: what simony is and how it is named; whether it is a sin; through whom it is committed (manu, lingua, obsequio); penalties for simoniacs; restitution of things simoniacally received; dispensation in such cases; and species of simony, including the case where the mediator alone is admitted. Throughout, Astesanus weighs civil and canon law authorities — Hostiensis, Goffredus, the *Decretum* and *Decretales*, and the *Glossa* — making this titulus a compact handbook of the simony tradition as received in the early fourteenth century.
