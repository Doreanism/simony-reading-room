Bonaventure's commentary on Distinction 25 of the fourth book of Peter Lombard's *Sentences*, which treats the conferral of holy orders. Article 1 asks who can confer orders, working through whether bishops alone hold the power, whether a heretical bishop can validly ordain, whether a simoniac bishop can ordain, and whether the act of ordaining simoniacally itself makes one a heretic. Article 2 examines questions about the recipient of orders. The Dubia at the end of the distinction include the influential Dub. VI on the modes of simony, asking *quibus modis contrahitur simonia* and unpacking the mnemonic verse *Nummus, lingua, timor, caro cum fama populari / Non faciunt gratis spirituale dari* — the gift of hand, tongue, fear, flesh, and favor.
