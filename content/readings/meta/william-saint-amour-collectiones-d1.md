A passage from the first distinction of William of Saint-Amour's *Collectiones* treating the *Opitulationes* — the auxiliary or "helper" offices in the Church. William argues that those who exercise such helping roles outside the ordinary hierarchy of bishops and parish priests lack proper jurisdiction over their hearers, and that the sustenance they receive from preaching is therefore not owed *potestative* (by right) but only *mendicative* (by begging for God's sake).
