In this letter of 3 March 1075, Gregory VII writes to the people of Lodi to applaud their zeal against simony and clerical incontinence and to urge them to support their bishop Opizo in extirpating these "pests" from their church. Issued within days of the 1075 Lent Synod (Reg. II.52a), the letter expressly invokes the synodal program: no one is to serve at the altar over the relics of St. Bassianus in the cathedral if he has entered the church by purchase, is a fornicator, or is a pursuer of base gain. Apostolic blessing is promised to those who cooperate with Opizo, apostolic curse to those who oppose him.
