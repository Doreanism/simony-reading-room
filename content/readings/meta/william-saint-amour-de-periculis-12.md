Chapter 12 of William of Saint-Amour's *De Periculis Novissimorum Temporum* (1256), addressing how the foregoing dangers of the last times are to be repelled. William enumerates six things necessary for repelling them, then mounts an extended argument against perpetual mendicancy by preachers — refuting the claim that Christ and the apostles begged, and concluding that withdrawing the means of mendicancy from pseudo-preachers also withdraws their usurped office of preaching.
