The *Summa Halensis*'s treatise on simony (*Titulus II: De Simonia*), part of the broader treatment of vices opposed to the gifts of the Holy Spirit. The treatise is divided into seven *capita*: (1) the name of simony; (2) what it is; (3) the modes in which it is contracted; (4) its material differences; (5) the penalty of simony; (6) doubtful cases; and (7) satisfaction and restitution. Composed by Alexander's Franciscan students in the 1240s and contemporary with the early teaching of Bonaventure, this is the foundational early Franciscan synthesis on simony, parallel to and predating Aquinas's *Summa Theologiae* II-II, Q. 100.
