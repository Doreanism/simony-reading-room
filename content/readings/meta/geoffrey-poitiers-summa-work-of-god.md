A three-column chain of quaestiones from Geoffrey of Poitiers' Summa working out, under the umbrella of Jeremiah 48:10 (*maledictus qui facit opus dei negligenter*), what it means to do God's work the wrong way. Geoffrey moves through three species — opus dei done **negligently** (folio 39ra), done **fraudulently** (39rb), and done **for money** (39va) — culminating in the celebrated quaestio on whether the master who teaches theology for fees falls under the curse of Micah 3:11, *sacerdotes eorum in mercede docebant*. There he draws the classic Parisian distinction that publicly licensed teachers (*publice persone*) are bound to teach freely as *doctores verbi dei*, lest they become *venditores verbi dei*.
