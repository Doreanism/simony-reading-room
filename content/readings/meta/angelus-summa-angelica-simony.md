The article on simony from Angelus de Clavasio's *Summa angelica de casibus conscientiae*, the alphabetically arranged confessor's manual first printed in 1486. Opening on folio 313v with a decorated initial immediately after the brief *Sigillum* article, the entry treats simony across twenty-six numbered sections: its definition as the deliberate buying or selling of something spiritual or annexed to the spiritual; the three modes by which it is committed — *a manu, a lingua, ab obsequio*; simoniacal ordination, promotion, and the conferral of benefices; the standing of sacraments received from simoniacs; penalties, irregularity, and the conditions for dispensation. Throughout, Angelus digests the canon-law tradition — the *Decretum*, the *Decretals*, and the major glossators and doctors — into practical cases of conscience for the confessor.
