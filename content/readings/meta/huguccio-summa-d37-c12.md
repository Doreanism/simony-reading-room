A short gloss-cluster from Huguccio's *Summa* on D. 37, c. 12 (*De quibusdam locis*), turning a *casus* about teachers and students into one of the earliest decretist treatments of selling the *licentia docendi* as simony. Huguccio asks first whether a master who refuses a cleric leave to attend school unless paid commits simony, then — *similiter* — whether the chancellor who sells the license to teach commits simony, recalling the practice "quod parisius olim fiebat" and the reforming decretals *Quanto Gallicana* (X 5.5.3) and *Quoniam ecclesia Dei* (X 5.5.1). He concludes that the act is at the very least *turpe lucrum* and *peccatum mortale*, and *forte* outright simony where the license concerns sacred learning such as *theologia*.
