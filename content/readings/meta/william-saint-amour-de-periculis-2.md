Chapter 2 of William of Saint-Amour's *De Periculis Novissimorum Temporum* (1256), identifying the kind of men through whom the dangers of the last times will come. Drawing on 2 Timothy 3, William catalogues the self-loving, "house-penetrating" preachers — the *penetrantes domos* — who claim a right to preach without being canonically sent, and argues from Scripture and canon law that such men are to be reckoned as pseudo-apostles.
