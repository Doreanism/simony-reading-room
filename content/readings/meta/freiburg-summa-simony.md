The opening of the first title of John of Freiburg's *Summa confessorum*, *De symonia*. After a brief preface noting that simoniacal heresy holds first place among ecclesiastical crimes, John proceeds through sixteen questions in the scholastic *quaestio* form: what simony is (the *studiosa voluntas emendi vel vendendi aliquid spirituale vel annexum spirituali*), the three kinds of gift by which it is committed (*a manu, a lingua, ab obsequio*), ordination and promotion for money, and related cases of conscience. Throughout, John digests and weighs the authorities — Raymond of Penyafort, Hostiensis, Goffredus of Trani, the *Glossa*, and Thomas Aquinas — whose names are keyed in the margins, exemplifying the method that made the *Summa confessorum* the standard confessor's manual of the later Middle Ages.
